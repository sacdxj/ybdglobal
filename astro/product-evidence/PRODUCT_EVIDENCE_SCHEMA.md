# Product Evidence Schema

Create one record per publishable SKU or configurable product platform.

## Identity

- internal SKU/model
- public name
- product family
- status and owner
- source document and review date

## Configuration

- connectors or port matrix
- protocol/version
- power profile
- bandwidth/display profile
- length options and limits
- jacket, housing, shell and materials
- colors and branding options
- packaging options

## Evidence

- test method and report reference
- certificate/report number
- covered model and market
- product images and diagrams
- known limitations

## Commercial confirmation

- MOQ basis
- sample availability/cost
- tooling or NRE
- lead-time basis
- warranty/policy reference

Every public claim must point to a field or evidence reference in this record. Missing values remain private-review and route to confirmation during RFQ.
