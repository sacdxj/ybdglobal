# Positioning

## Category

B2B connectivity and charging accessories supplier/manufacturer platform for USB cables, HDMI cables, DisplayPort cables, USB-C hubs, docking stations and chargers.

## Commercial position

The website is not primarily a retail store and should not lead with “Buy Now.” It is a Google acquisition and RFQ qualification system for buyers who need specifications, customization, evidence, samples and commercial confirmation before ordering.

## Positioning statement

> For wholesalers, importers, electronics brands and private-label sellers sourcing connectivity and charging accessories, this project provides a specification-first path from product selection to a quote-ready requirement, with claims held behind product and market evidence.

## Differentiation

- One sourcing path across cables, hubs, docks and chargers
- Product + specification + buyer-intent SEO architecture
- Configurator-led RFQ preparation
- Evidence before protocol, performance, factory or certification claims
- OEM/ODM requirements captured before quotation

## Not the position

- A generic “China factory” brochure
- A consumer troubleshooting site
- A marketplace affiliate site
- A retail catalog with unsupported prices or stock
- A site that creates one page for every keyword variant

## Expansion boundary

Adapters, power strips and adjacent laptop/mobile accessories may be added only when product evidence, business ownership and keyword demand justify a coherent new product family.
