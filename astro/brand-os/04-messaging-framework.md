# Messaging Framework

## Message hierarchy

### 1. What is supplied

USB, HDMI and DisplayPort cables, hubs, docking stations and charging accessories for B2B sourcing programs.

### 2. Why the buying process is different

Price depends on the exact interface, performance, length, construction, customization, evidence and quantity. Configure first; quote second.

### 3. Why trust the process

Protocol, performance, certification, factory and commercial claims are not published as facts until supporting evidence is available.

### 4. What buyers can do

Get a quote, configure a product, request a sample, discuss private label or request an evidence pack.

## Voice

- Precise, commercial and technically literate
- Direct about what is confirmed and what remains project-dependent
- Useful to procurement without pretending every buyer is an engineer
- Confident about process, conservative about unsupported facts

## Avoid

- “Best quality,” “lowest price,” “leading factory” or “world-class” without proof
- Keyword-stuffed manufacturer/supplier/factory repetition
- Consumer hype and unexplained protocol badges
- Fixed MOQ, capacity, lead time or certification claims inferred from industry norms
- “Buy Now” before stock, price, payment and fulfillment operations exist
