# Claim and Evidence System

## Evidence classes

### Product identity

SKU/model, connector/port configuration, materials, dimensions and packaging.

### Technical performance

Protocol/version, wattage, current, voltage, bandwidth, resolution, refresh rate and length-dependent performance.

### Compliance

Certificate, report number, issuing body, model coverage, market and validity date.

### Manufacturing

Legal production entity, facility role, process, equipment, quality checkpoints and capacity source.

### Commercial

MOQ, sample cost, tooling, price, payment, lead time, warranty, returns and shipping.

## Publication states

- `draft`: structure or wording incomplete
- `private-review`: usable internally; evidence or identity incomplete
- `evidence-approved`: required claims trace to reviewed sources
- `publish-ready`: content, legal, SEO and operational gates approved

## Safe fallback

When evidence is absent, say “confirm by selected SKU,” “project option,” or “request the project evidence pack.” Do not turn a possible option into a public promise.
