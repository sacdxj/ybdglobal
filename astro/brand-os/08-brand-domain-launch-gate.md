# Brand, Domain and Launch Gate

## Current blockers

- final brand name
- domain ownership
- trademark/conflict screening
- legal company identity and address
- public email/phone and RFQ receiver
- privacy and form-processing details
- product and factory evidence
- market-specific certifications
- warranty, returns and logistics policies

## Private-review requirements

- `PUBLIC_NOINDEX=true`
- robots disallow all
- working name clearly labeled provisional in internal documentation
- no live form without a verified receiver and privacy disclosure
- no unsupported offer, Product or certification schema

## Public-launch requirements

1. Approve name, domain and company identity.
2. Replace working identity across source, metadata, Schema, assets and environment files.
3. Approve RFQ receiver, consent, privacy and retention workflow.
4. Approve evidence for every public product and factory claim.
5. Approve legal and commercial policies.
6. Run build, link, legacy-leak and public-launch checks.
7. Only then change indexing and submit the sitemap.

The public-launch check is expected to fail while `Connectivity Supply` or `.invalid` remains active.
