# Buyer Personas

## Wholesaler

Needs assortment breadth, price bands, MOQ, mixed-product sourcing, packaging consistency, replenishment and margin potential. Primary CTA: `Get Bulk Pricing`.

## Importer / distributor

Needs destination-market compliance, product documentation, packaging language, shipment planning, stable supply and forecast support. Primary CTA: `Discuss Supply Program`.

## Electronics brand

Needs OEM/ODM feasibility, engineering communication, differentiation, product evidence, controlled changes and repeatable quality. Primary CTA: `Start Customization`.

## Amazon / marketplace seller

Needs private label, barcode and packaging execution, samples, compliance evidence, launch timing and consistent specifications. Primary CTA: `Discuss Private Label`.

## Retailer

Needs clear assortment, packaging-ready SKUs, product claims that can be substantiated, warranty workflow and replenishment. Primary CTA: `Request Product Range`.

## Procurement questions every page should anticipate

- Is this the right product/specification for the market?
- What exactly can be customized?
- What evidence supports the performance claim?
- What must be confirmed before price and lead time?
- Can I obtain a representative sample?
- What information will make the RFQ actionable?
