# USB Connectivity Brand OS

## Current status

`Connectivity Supply` is a private-review working identity, not an approved public brand. The final name, domain, legal company identity and trademark clearance remain launch blockers.

## Operating idea

Build a B2B sourcing system that turns Google demand into a specification-complete RFQ:

`Search → choose product family → define specification → evaluate supply proof → configure customization → submit RFQ → sales follow-up`

## Source-of-truth order

1. Approved company, product, test, certificate and policy evidence
2. Structured product data in `src/data/products.ts`
3. Publishable page metadata and editorial copy in `src/content/site/`
4. Keyword-to-page architecture in `../outputs/usb-seo/usb_connectivity_keyword_architecture_full.xlsx`
5. Planning and evidence requirements in `../site-content/`
