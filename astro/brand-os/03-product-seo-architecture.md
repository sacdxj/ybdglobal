# Product and SEO Architecture

## Three independent layers

### Product

- USB cables
- HDMI cables
- DisplayPort cables
- USB-C hubs and docking stations
- USB-C, PD and GaN chargers

Product category pages target category demand and help buyers choose a product platform. They must not use manufacturer intent as their primary keyword.

### Specification

Create a standalone page only when the specification represents a distinct buyer task, product set and likely SERP intent. Examples include USB4, 100W/240W USB-C, HDMI 2.1, fiber/AOC HDMI, DP 1.4/2.1, high-refresh DisplayPort and high-power chargers.

Do not publish a specification page until at least one supported product path exists. A planned URL may remain unpublished while evidence is missing.

### Buyer intent

- Manufacturer / supplier / factory
- Wholesale / bulk
- OEM / ODM / custom / private label
- Wholesaler, importer, distributor, brand, Amazon seller and retailer

Manufacturer, supplier and factory variants normally belong to one landing page per product family. Split only after SERP validation shows materially different user tasks.

## Cannibalization rules

- Home targets the broad multi-category supplier position.
- Product category targets the product name.
- Manufacturer landing targets procurement-source intent.
- Specification page targets the confirmed specification.
- Buyer solution targets one buyer type and its workflow.
- Resource content supports a parent commercial page and does not replace it.

## URL policy

Use stable, descriptive, lowercase, trailing-slash URLs. URL nesting may express product hierarchy, but search intent—not menu convenience—decides whether a page exists.
