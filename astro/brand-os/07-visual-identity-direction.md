# Visual Identity Direction

## Status

The current `Connectivity Supply` name, CS mark and visual execution are working materials for private review. They are not cleared brand assets.

## Direction

Use a precision-industrial sourcing aesthetic:

- deep blue-black technical base
- restrained copper/orange signal color
- off-white documentation surfaces
- specification labels, connector geometry and controlled linework
- editorial hierarchy rather than generic SaaS cards

## Visual purpose

The interface should make product differences, evidence state and RFQ steps easier to understand. Decoration must support technical orientation and procurement confidence.

## Product media requirements

- clean product hero
- connector close-ups
- construction/material detail
- packaging and customization example
- dimensional or port-layout diagram
- test/evidence image where approved

Do not use electric dirt bike imagery, generic factory stock photos or certification logos without permission and product applicability.
