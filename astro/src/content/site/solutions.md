## Choose the buying program

The product may be similar, but evidence, packaging, forecast, and approval requirements change by buyer type. Use this page to select an intake path—not as proof of supply capability.

| Buyer program | Information to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| [Wholesale and distribution](/solutions/wholesalers-distributors/) | Product-family mix, quantity bands, destination markets, packaging, logistics, and replenishment plan |
| [Brands and private label](/solutions/brands-private-label/) | Product brief, identity system, packaging, barcode, evidence, and sample approval process |
| [Retail and ecommerce](/solutions/retail-ecommerce/) | Channel assortment, listing claims, packaging, launch timing, and replenishment workflow |

## Common approval gates

Every program should confirm the exact product configuration, destination market, evidence list, forecast, sample need, and target date. MOQ, price, lead time, certification, and production claims remain project-dependent.

## The same product needs different sales logic

A distributor, a private-label brand and an ecommerce operator may all ask for a USB-C cable, but they are not buying the same project. The distributor needs a sellable range and replenishment logic. The private-label brand needs revision control, package approval and claim discipline. The ecommerce team needs listing language, compatibility evidence and launch timing.

| Buyer type | Main risk to solve | RFQ should clarify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wholesale and distribution | Wrong assortment mix, unclear quantity breaks and poor replenishment planning | Product-family mix, forecast, packaging tier, destination market and reorder logic |
| Brands and private label | Artwork and product claims drifting away from the approved sample | Product revision, brand assets, packaging files, evidence scope and golden-sample approval |
| Retail and ecommerce | Listing claims, package icons or compatibility statements creating returns and compliance risk | Channel, title claims, compatibility language, launch date, sample owner and market evidence |

## What happens before price

Price is useful only after the scope is comparable. Before commercial terms are confirmed, the project should define product task, configuration, quantity, forecast, market, packaging, evidence and sample approval. If any of these remain open, the quotation should show the missing decision rather than pretend the project is already fixed.
