## Buyer situation

A private-label buyer needed a charger SKU with brand packaging for a specific market. The project depended on more than box artwork: plug format, output behavior, safety markings, package claims and channel requirements all needed review.

## Customization scope

| Area | Project requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Charger reference | Confirm output requirement, port count, plug market and use case before package claims are written |
| Logo and marking | Review body marking, label content or package-only branding depending on surface and regulatory constraints |
| Packaging | Prepare color box, insert, barcode, SKU label, carton mark and sales-channel wording |
| Evidence | Match safety, power and compatibility claims to the selected charger model and destination market |

## Approval workflow

Start with the electrical and market requirement, then review package artwork and marking requirements. A packed sample should be checked before final commercial terms are locked, especially when sales-channel text or safety claims appear on the box.

## RFQ inputs to prepare

Send target wattage, port mix, plug market, logo file, package reference, destination country, sales channel and required evidence list. If certificates or test reports are required, identify the exact market and claim scope.

> **Review status:** Anonymous case pattern. Do not publish charger safety, certification, MOQ, lead time or channel compliance statements without approved evidence.
