## Buyer situation

A private-label buyer needed a USB-C cable program that could move from a reference cable to a retail-ready pack. The important work was not only the cable itself, but the packaging, barcode, SKU label, compatibility wording and approval record.

## Customization scope

| Area | Project requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Product reference | Select the cable family, connector ends, color, length and visible construction before artwork starts |
| Logo and identity | Confirm whether the brand appears on cable, connector shell, packaging, insert or shipping label |
| Retail pack | Review color box or kraft box direction, barcode, SKU label, manual and carton mark |
| Evidence | Keep charging, data, material and compatibility claims pending until the exact sample and claim list are reviewed |

## Approval workflow

1. Confirm the reference cable and required differences.
2. Review logo placement and package structure against the selected model.
3. Prepare artwork files for buyer review.
4. Produce or review a packed sample before mass production terms are locked.
5. Carry the approved product revision, artwork revision and claim list into the final RFQ.

## RFQ inputs to prepare

Send the cable model or product family, target length, color, logo file, packaging reference, barcode or SKU requirements, destination market, channel and required evidence. Price, MOQ and timing should be quoted after these inputs are reviewed.
