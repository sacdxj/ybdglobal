## Build the channel brief before selecting claims

Define the target channel, customer task, product-family mix, price architecture, launch date, packaging format, and replenishment model. The product and evidence must support the claims used on the shelf, package, product detail page, and marketplace listing.

## The channel changes the product brief

Retail shelves, ecommerce listings and marketplace bundles each need a different level of claim discipline. The same USB-C cable or charger may need different package hierarchy, image requirements, compatibility wording, barcode handling and sample review depending on where it will be sold.

| Channel pressure | Content and sourcing implication |
| --- | --- |
| Retail shelf | Package front claims must be simple, supportable and visible at a glance |
| Ecommerce listing | Title, bullets, compatibility and images must match the selected product evidence |
| Marketplace launch | Sample review should consider return risk, claim wording and variation structure |
| Replenishment program | Forecast, reorder timing and packaging consistency matter after launch |

## Retail and ecommerce checklist

- exact product configuration and intended customer task;
- channel assortment, variant logic, initial quantity, and forecast;
- product title, feature hierarchy, compatibility language, and required evidence;
- retail or shipment-ready packaging, barcode, labels, manuals, and imagery requirements;
- sample, listing, packaging, and golden-sample approval owners;
- launch date, reorder signals, destination market, and logistics constraints.

## Claim and launch gate

Listing copy, package icons, compatibility statements, certifications, availability, price, and lead time remain provisional until the selected configuration, evidence, and commercial terms are approved. [Prepare the retail RFQ](/get-a-quote/?program=Private%20label).

## Better RFQ input

Send the channel, intended customer task, product-family mix, package type, listing or shelf claims, quantity, forecast, destination market, launch date and sample approval plan. If the claims are not final, mark them as requested claims for evidence review.
