## From inquiry to shipment: a controlled OEM data cable project

On October 10, an overseas customer contacted us about an **OEM data cable** project. The buyer needed more than a standard cable order: the project included packaging confirmation, a custom logo, an artwork preview, delivery coordination and photos of the finished goods before shipment.

For importers, distributors and private-label brands, these details are essential. A cable may be suitable for the intended sales program, but unclear artwork or packaging instructions can still delay an order. We therefore treated each approval as a separate step and kept the communication traceable.

## Confirming the packaging and custom logo

Our team first clarified the requested packaging presentation and logo placement. The customer's branding request was then translated into a visual mockup so the proposed result could be reviewed before production.

The mockup was sent to the customer for confirmation. This gave the buyer an opportunity to check the logo position, packaging appearance and visible information before the order moved forward. If a buyer requests a change, the artwork stage is the right point to correct it rather than waiting until the goods are packed.

This review process is part of our **OEM and ODM data cable** workflow. Available customization depends on the selected cable and packaging format, so logo application, labels, barcodes and other private-label elements are confirmed project by project.

## Coordinating production and delivery timing

After the artwork and packaging details were approved, the order proceeded according to the confirmed requirements. The delivery schedule was coordinated with the customer so both sides had a clear reference for production, packing and shipment preparation.

We do not treat lead time as a universal promise. It is confirmed for each project according to the selected product, quantity, customization work and destination requirements.

## Sending finished-goods photos before shipment

When the products were packed, photos of the finished order were sent to the customer for another check. The images showed the actual data cables inside their individual branded packages and the packed goods prepared for dispatch.

## What this project demonstrates

The advantage shown in this case is a practical approval process: requirements were clarified, custom-logo artwork was reviewed, packaging details were confirmed, delivery timing was coordinated, and finished-goods photos were shared before dispatch.

Buyers planning a similar project can send the cable type, connector combination, target length, quantity, destination market, logo file, packaging requirements and target delivery date. Product performance, certification, MOQ, price and lead time are confirmed for the selected SKU and quotation.

This case documents one project workflow. It does not establish specifications, capacity or commercial terms for future orders.
