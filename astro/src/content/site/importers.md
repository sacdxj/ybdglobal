## Define the market before the product claim

Identify destination countries, sales channels, required languages, importer responsibilities, and the documents your compliance team expects. Certification statements must be mapped to the selected model and market.

## Import sourcing begins with destination risk

For importers and regional distributors, the same product can require different labels, manuals, certificate scope, carton marks and document review depending on the destination market. The sourcing discussion should identify the market and importer responsibilities before any compliance or shipping promise is treated as approved.

| Import decision | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Destination country | Drives label, manual, plug, language and compliance review |
| Importer role | Clarifies who needs which records before shipment |
| Channel | Wholesale, retail and ecommerce require different packaging and claim discipline |
| Document list | Certificates, reports and product identity must match the selected model |
| Shipment timing | Availability and lead time remain project-confirmed until scope is reviewed |

## Import-program checklist

- model-level product and performance specification;
- destination-market certificate and report requirements;
- label, manual, barcode, packaging, and carton language;
- trial order, forecast, shipment preference, and target date;
- sample, document, and pre-shipment approval workflow.

## Evidence and logistics gate

No certificate, shipping, lead-time, warranty, or capacity statement is treated as approved until the operating company provides current evidence and policy. [Prepare the importer RFQ](/get-a-quote/?program=Import%20%2F%20distribution).

## Better RFQ input

Send the destination market, importer requirements, product family, model task, label/manual needs, packaging, carton requirements, quantity, forecast, sample need and document checklist. Treat every certificate, logistics and timing statement as pending until current evidence is matched.
