A wholesale customer received a cable shipment for a phone accessories order and shared delivery photos through chat. The first update confirmed that the cartons had arrived, and the customer later checked the contents before testing the products.

## Delivery and package check

After the goods arrived, the customer confirmed that the cables were included and that the package condition was acceptable. We reminded the customer to inspect the goods and report any damaged box, so replacement packaging could be arranged in the next order if needed.

This after-sales communication is important for wholesale and OEM/ODM cable projects because packaging condition, carton protection and product inspection all affect repeat orders.

## Product testing feedback

After checking the package and testing the products, the customer gave a short but valuable quality comment:

> "They are very good quality."

## Why buyers care

For wholesale cable and phone accessories buyers, a good supplier is not only judged by the product listing. Buyers also need stable delivery communication, safe package handling, responsive after-sales support and a clear process for future OEM/ODM requirements such as custom logo, packaging, carton labels and repeat-order planning.

YBD Global keeps this type of feedback in the project record so future cable orders can be reviewed with better packaging notes, product inspection feedback and customer service follow-up.
