# Website Design System

This document defines reusable page structures for the Axiterm / Binding Post website. It turns the procurement-decision audit into repeatable page templates so new pages do not drift in structure, conversion logic, or buyer intent.

## 1. Design Goal

Every page should work as part of the buyer journey:

1. Help the visitor identify whether the page fits their project.
2. Reduce procurement or engineering risk.
3. Provide decision evidence.
4. Add useful information beyond generic manufacturer claims.
5. Move the visitor to the next logical page or RFQ action.

The site should remain OEM B2B manufacturing focused, with Binding Post as the buyer-recognition and SEO entry point, Precision Terminal Components Manufacturer as the brand ceiling, and RFQ / engineering review as the conversion goal.

## 2. Global Page Rules

- Keep hero copy specific: what Axiterm makes, who it serves, and what project problem the page solves.
- Use `Request a Quote`, `Upload Drawing`, `Send Drawing for Review`, or `Request Manufacturing Review` instead of generic `Contact Us` for high-intent pages.
- Do not add unsupported claims such as ISO, tolerance numbers, country counts, years, or lead times unless they are verified.
- Use conceptual images only as placeholders. Replace with real product, factory, inspection, packaging, or project evidence when assets are available.
- Each page must have a clear next step.
- Page sections should be full-width bands or unframed layouts. Use cards for repeated items, checklists, modals, and framed tools.

## 3. Homepage Template

Purpose: positioning, segmentation, trust entry, and RFQ routing.

Required structure:

1. Hero: H1, product/application/manufacturing subtitle, RFQ CTA, quick routing.
2. Product Entry Points: Binding Posts first, then Banana Plugs, Spade Connectors, High Current Terminals, Custom Terminal Components.
3. Applications: Audio first, then Test & Measurement, Power Equipment, Industrial Equipment.
4. Manufacturing Capabilities: CNC, brass/copper processing, injection molded insulation, assembly/inspection.
5. Decision Evidence: engineering review, manufacturing route, production control.
6. RFQ Trust: drawing understanding, commercial risk, repeat-order control.
7. RFQ Conversion Path: what buyer sends, what Axiterm reviews, what controls production.
8. Final CTA.

Pass condition: a new visitor can understand Axiterm's positioning and choose the next path within one screen.

## 4. Product Hub Template

Purpose: help buyers choose the closest product family.

Required structure:

1. Hero.
2. Product Decision Guide: if buyer has this sourcing problem, use this page.
3. Product Lines: fit, key RFQ checks, and next step.
4. Custom Manufacturing Paths.
5. RFQ Input Reminder.
6. CTA.

Pass condition: the page helps a buyer decide where to click, not just what categories exist.

## 5. Product Detail Template

Purpose: help engineers and purchasing teams judge product fit before RFQ.

Required structure:

1. Hero: product family, application scope, project review language.
2. Product or concept visual.
3. Quick Specs.
4. Product Variants.
5. Technical Specifications.
6. Buyer Checklist by role.
7. Information Gain: material route, finish route, interface risk, failure risk, or selection guidance.
8. Manufacturing Capabilities.
9. Applications and related links.
10. Evidence / Specification Checklist.
11. FAQ.
12. CTA.

Pass condition: the page helps the buyer decide whether to request quote, send drawing, or move to a custom route.

## 6. Custom Manufacturing Page Template

Purpose: route drawing-based, sample-based, or undefined OEM projects.

Required structure:

1. Hero with Upload Drawing CTA and project control path.
2. Engineering review, sample, drawing, or inspection visual.
3. Scope / component types.
4. Decision Matrix: modify existing part, replace supplier part, new OEM development, material-driven route.
5. Project Inputs.
6. Control Points.
7. Production Control: sample basis, change control, repeat order control.
8. Related Manufacturing Paths.
9. CTA.

Pass condition: a buyer with an unclear or custom project knows which information to send and which route applies.

## 7. Application Page Template

Purpose: let visitors choose by equipment type and then move toward product or RFQ.

Required structure:

1. Hero with equipment application and interface problem.
2. Application Fit.
3. Component Scope.
4. Application-specific RFQ Review.
5. Buyer Confidence / Evidence.
6. Product next-step links.
7. CTA.

Pass condition: the page proves Axiterm understands the application, not just the product name.

## 8. Capability Page Template

Purpose: prove a manufacturing route is suitable for terminal component projects.

Hub required structure:

1. Hero.
2. Capability Pages.
3. Project Review Process.
4. Procurement Questions.
5. Evidence band when assets exist.
6. CTA.

Detail required structure:

1. Hero.
2. Capability Scope.
3. RFQ Inputs.
4. Manufacturing Risks to Control.
5. Decision Evidence: machines, process, inspection, material behavior, sample approval, packaging, or documents.
6. Related Pages.
7. CTA.

Pass condition: the page answers what can be verified through this capability and what information is needed for review.

## 9. RFQ Template

Purpose: convert qualified visitors into useful RFQs.

Required structure:

1. Hero: RFQ scope and no-finished-drawing option.
2. Best RFQ Inputs.
3. Quote Path.
4. What Happens Next.
5. Before You Submit.
6. RFQ Form.
7. Buyer Confidence.
8. Offer Strategy.
9. After Submission.
10. FAQ / contact fallback.

Pass condition: the form collects enough data for engineering review without making the buyer feel lost.

## 10. Resource Page Template

Purpose: build information gain and topical authority.

Use for future Engineering Resources:

- Material Guide.
- Tolerance Guide.
- Current Guide.
- Thread Guide.
- Surface Finish Guide.
- Packaging Guide.
- Inspection Guide.

Required structure:

1. Search-intent hero.
2. Practical decision framework.
3. Tables or checklists.
4. Common failure risks.
5. Product/application links.
6. RFQ relevance.
7. FAQ.

Pass condition: the page teaches something useful and routes serious buyers back to product, capability, or RFQ pages.

## 11. Layout Verification

After template changes:

- Check desktop and mobile layout.
- Confirm cards do not become nested cards.
- Confirm CTA buttons have stable text and do not overflow.
- Confirm headings are readable and not hero-sized inside compact cards.
- Confirm conceptual images are labeled as conceptual unless replaced by real assets.
