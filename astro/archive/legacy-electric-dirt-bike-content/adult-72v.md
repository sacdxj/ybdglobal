## What Voltage Tells You

Voltage describes one electrical characteristic of the battery system. It can help buyers
compare architectures, but it does not show:

- How much energy the battery stores.
- How the controller limits or delivers current.
- Motor efficiency or sustained-load behavior.
- Gearing and wheel-size effects.
- Bike weight or rider fit.
- Braking, suspension, tires, or chassis quality.
- Real range under the rider's conditions.

That is why a 72V label should open a comparison, not finish it.

## Compare the Complete Drive System

| Specification | Question it should answer |
|---|---|
| Nominal voltage | What system architecture does the bike use? |
| Battery capacity | How much stored energy is documented? |
| Motor rating | Is the figure nominal, peak, or another defined condition? |
| Controller | How is power delivered and limited? |
| Gearing/wheel size | How does the system trade acceleration, speed, and control? |
| Thermal guidance | What limits apply during sustained load or high temperature? |
| Charger | Is it approved for this exact battery, and what electrical input is required? |

Read the model page for current source specs and avoid comparing 72V bikes by voltage alone.

## Who Should Consider a 72V Bike?

A 72V model may belong on the shortlist when:

- The rider has the physical fit and experience for the complete bike.
- The intended terrain benefits from the model's documented performance characteristics.
- Brakes, suspension, tires, and chassis match the use.
- The buyer understands charging, transport, maintenance, and support.
- The price and ownership requirements fit the budget.

It may be the wrong starting point when the rider is new, the bike is physically difficult to
manage, the riding area is limited, or the buyer is choosing by voltage alone.

## 72V Buyer Checklist

- Confirm the exact nominal voltage and battery capacity.
- Confirm motor/controller definitions and limits.
- Check rider fit and complete bike weight.
- Review brakes, suspension, tires, and intended terrain.
- Read the speed and range test method.
- Confirm charger compatibility and electrical requirements.
- Check parts, warranty, shipping, and service.

## FAQ

### Is 72V better than 60V?

Not universally. The better system is the one whose complete bike fits the rider, use, budget,
charging plan, and support expectations.

### Is every 72V electric dirt bike fast?

No conclusion should be drawn from voltage alone. Verify complete-system performance using a
documented test method.

### Can a 72V bike be street legal?

Voltage does not determine legal classification. Verify the exact product documentation,
equipment, and applicable state/local requirements.
