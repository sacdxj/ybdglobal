## Battery and Charging

The source table lists a 48V 20.8Ah domestic lithium battery with 1000Wh capacity. Charger
options are 3A CE-certified and 5A CE-certified. Charging time is listed as 5 hours with the 3A
charger and 4 hours with the 5A charger.

Confirm battery label, charger input/output, storage guidance, replacement-battery policy, and
range assumptions before purchase.

## Ride and Chassis

The K2-48-MX-1214 uses an iron frame, spray paint finish, inverted aluminum front suspension,
domestic hydraulic rear shock absorber, and 2-piston cast iron disc brakes. Headlight and
taillight are optional in the source table.

The public page should clearly state the intended terrain, assembly requirements, brake service
interval, tire pressure, rider-size guidance, and protective-gear expectations.
