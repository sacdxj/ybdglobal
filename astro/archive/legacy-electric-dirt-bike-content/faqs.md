# FAQs

Use these answers to reduce purchase risk before checkout. For exact model fit, start with Find Your Bike and then confirm policy details before ordering.

<div class="support-brief">
  <article><span>Choose By Rider</span><p>Fit, maturity, terrain, supervision, and control confidence matter more than age alone.</p></article>
  <article><span>Confirm Real Costs</span><p>Review price, shipping, tax, gear, maintenance, financing, returns, and warranty together.</p></article>
  <article><span>Inspect Delivery</span><p>Photograph packaging and product before assembly, charging, or riding.</p></article>
  <article><span>Legal Use Varies</span><p>Do not assume street legality. State, local, equipment, and documentation rules matter.</p></article>
</div>

<div class="support-faq-grid">
  <details open><summary>Which Ovkara bike should I choose?</summary><p>Start with rider class, height, seat height, wheel size, experience, terrain, speed comfort, range expectations, supervision needs, and support requirements. Use <a href="/#find-your-bike">Find Your Bike</a> before comparing models.</p></details>
  <details><summary>What is the safest way to choose a kids or youth bike?</summary><p>Do not choose by age alone. Confirm rider size, maturity, throttle control, braking confidence, supervision, PPE, terrain, speed limits, and local rules.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Should I choose 48V, 60V, or 72V?</summary><p>Voltage is one signal, not the whole decision. Compare rider skill, terrain, wheel size, motor output, battery capacity, control modes, brakes, suspension, weight, and support path.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Are speed and range numbers guaranteed?</summary><p>No. Speed and range depend on rider weight, terrain, temperature, tire pressure, battery state, mode, surface, elevation, wind, maintenance, and test method.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Can I order now?</summary><p>Yes, when the product page is active and the model is available. Confirm rider fit, delivery ZIP, shipping terms, warranty, returns, and support path first.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Are prices final?</summary><p>Prices must be confirmed on the product page, quote, or checkout.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Can I finance a purchase?</summary><p>Financing depends on the provider, eligibility, product, jurisdiction, APR, fees, credit process, and refund handling. Review <a href="/support/financing/">Financing</a> before applying.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Where do you ship?</summary><p>Standard checkout supports eligible destinations within the contiguous United States. Contact support for special delivery questions.</p></details>
  <details><summary>What should I do when the bike arrives?</summary><p>Inspect before assembly, charging, or riding. Photograph the carton and product, keep packaging, confirm package count, and contact support if anything is damaged or missing.</p></details>
  <details><summary>What is the return policy?</summary><p>Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery. Returned products must be authorized, unused, clean, undamaged, unmodified, and complete with original packaging and accessories unless support approves an exception.</p></details>
  <details><summary>What does the warranty cover?</summary><p>Ovkara provides a 12-month limited warranty for eligible products purchased directly from Ovkara or an approved sales channel. Coverage depends on model, component, use case, and claim evidence.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Are Ovkara electric dirt bikes street legal?</summary><p>Do not assume street legality. Legal use depends on model, equipment, VIN/MSO/title status, registration, insurance, license requirements, state and local rules, and where the bike is used.</p></details>
</div>
