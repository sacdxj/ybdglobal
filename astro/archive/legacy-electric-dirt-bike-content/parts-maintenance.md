# Electric Dirt Bike Parts & Maintenance

Match every part to the exact model, serial range, and revision. Visual similarity is not enough to prove compatibility.

<div class="support-brief">
  <article><span>Exact Fit</span><p>Use model, serial number, revision, and photos before confirming a part.</p></article>
  <article><span>Wear Items</span><p>Tires, brakes, chain/drivetrain, controls, bearings, and fasteners need routine review.</p></article>
  <article><span>Battery Safety</span><p>Use approved batteries and chargers only. Connector fit is not enough.</p></article>
  <article><span>Manual First</span><p>Intervals, torque values, lubrication, and wear limits should come from the exact model manual.</p></article>
</div>

<div class="support-detail-grid">
  <section class="support-card">
    <h2>Common Part Categories</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Tires, tubes, wheels, bearings, and related hardware.</li>
      <li>Brake pads, rotors, levers, lines, and approved fluids/components.</li>
      <li>Chains, belts, sprockets, tension components, and guards.</li>
      <li>Suspension, chassis wear items, bodywork, seats, foot controls, and fasteners.</li>
      <li>Controls, displays, wiring, connectors, switches, approved batteries, and chargers.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="support-card support-card--dark">
    <h2>Parts Request Checklist</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Model and serial number.</li>
      <li>Order or purchase information.</li>
      <li>Part name, location, and product-label photos.</li>
      <li>Description of wear, damage, or symptom.</li>
      <li>Previous modifications, repairs, and shipping destination.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

<div class="support-detail-grid">
  <section class="support-card support-card--dark">
    <h2>Pre-Ride Inspection</h2>
    <p>A routine review may include tires, brakes, controls, fasteners, drive components, suspension, battery mounting, visible wiring, and warning indicators.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="support-card">
    <h2>Tire Selection</h2>
    <p>Tire width alone does not establish traction or terrain suitability. Review compound, tread, pressure, wheel, load, clearance, surface, and product approval.</p>
  </section>
</div>

<div class="support-faq-grid">
  <details><summary>Are electric dirt bike parts universal?</summary><p>No. Verify the exact model, serial range, and revision.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Can I use a charger with the same connector?</summary><p>No. Connector fit does not prove electrical or communication compatibility. Use an approved charger.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Where can I find service intervals?</summary><p>Use the exact model manual and approved support documentation.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Can I modify high-voltage parts?</summary><p>Do not open, rebuild, bypass, or modify battery, controller, motor, or charging components.</p></details>
</div>
