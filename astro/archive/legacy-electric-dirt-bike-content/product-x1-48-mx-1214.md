# X1-48-MX-1214 48V Compact Electric Dirt Bike

The X1-48-MX-1214 is the compact 48V model in the X1 lineup. It uses a 48V 20.8Ah lithium
battery, 1000Wh source capacity, 1000W rated mid-mounted motor, 12/14 tire setup, and a 730 mm
seat height.

It should be positioned as a compact step-up product, not as a kids recommendation.
