# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ovkara collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit this website, interact with our content, use our online services, contact support, request product information, or make a purchase from us.

For purposes of this Privacy Policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to Ovkara. "You" and "your" refer to visitors, shoppers, customers, owners, support contacts, and other users of the website.

By using the website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the website or submit personal information through it.

## Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices, services, technologies, legal requirements, or business operations. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

The updated Privacy Policy will be effective when posted on the website unless a different effective date is stated.

## Personal Information We Collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you interact with the website and our services.

## Information You Provide To Us

We may collect information that you provide directly, including:

- Name, email address, phone number, billing address, and shipping address.
- Account, order, purchase, payment, shipping, delivery, return, warranty, and support details.
- Product interest, model preferences, rider-fit information, riding use case, location, budget, and comparison questions.
- Messages, forms, reviews, survey responses, support requests, photos, videos, serial numbers, proof of purchase, and other documentation you choose to provide.
- Dealer, wholesale, partnership, or business inquiry details.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through general contact forms unless we specifically request it through an approved process.

## Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit the website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and activity, including:

- IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and approximate location.
- Pages viewed, links clicked, products viewed, search terms, referring URLs, session activity, and timestamps.
- Cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, analytics identifiers, advertising identifiers, and similar technologies.
- Decision Engine answers, product comparison activity, support clicks, and other website interaction data.

## Information From Third Parties

We may receive information from third parties that help us operate the website and business, such as:

- Ecommerce platforms, payment processors, financing providers, fraud-prevention services, and checkout providers.
- Shipping carriers, freight providers, warehouse partners, and delivery-service providers.
- Analytics, advertising, search, email, SMS, customer-support, review, and marketing providers.
- Social media platforms, business partners, affiliates, dealers, professional advisors, and publicly available sources.

## How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for the following purposes:

- To provide, operate, maintain, and improve the website and services.
- To help shoppers compare models and choose an electric dirt bike with more confidence.
- To process orders, payments, financing requests, shipping, delivery, cancellations, returns, refunds, warranty claims, and support requests.
- To communicate with you about orders, products, support, service, policy updates, safety information, and administrative matters.
- To personalize website content, improve product information, evaluate buyer questions, and improve the Decision Engine.
- To provide marketing communications, advertising, promotions, and offers where permitted by law.
- To measure website performance, advertising performance, SEO performance, email performance, and customer-support quality.
- To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, policy violations, disputes, and harmful activity.
- To comply with legal, tax, accounting, safety, regulatory, warranty, and dispute-resolution obligations.

## Cookies And Tracking Technologies

We and our service providers may use cookies, pixels, tags, scripts, analytics tools, advertising tools, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, measure performance, understand website use, improve content, and support advertising.

You can usually manage cookies through your browser settings. Some parts of the website may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

Depending on the tools used on the website, third-party analytics and advertising providers may also offer their own opt-out controls.

## How We Disclose Personal Information

We may disclose personal information to:

- Service providers and vendors that help operate the website, ecommerce platform, checkout, payments, financing, shipping, fulfillment, analytics, advertising, email, SMS, customer support, fraud prevention, hosting, security, and technical systems.
- Business and marketing partners where permitted by law.
- Shipping carriers, freight providers, warehouses, service partners, warranty partners, and repair partners as needed to fulfill or support your order.
- Payment processors and financing providers, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.
- Professional advisors, insurers, auditors, legal counsel, and other business-support providers.
- Government authorities, regulators, law enforcement, courts, or other parties when required or permitted by law.
- Relevant parties in connection with a business transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar transaction.

We do not intentionally disclose more information than is reasonably needed for the relevant business, legal, operational, or support purpose.

## Third-Party Websites And Services

The website may contain links to third-party websites, services, platforms, payment providers, financing providers, shipping providers, social media platforms, or other external resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or policies of third-party websites or services. You should review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

## Payment And Financing Information

Payments and financing may be handled by third-party providers. Those providers may collect, process, store, and protect payment, credit, financing, identity, fraud-prevention, and transaction information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Ovkara should not request or store complete payment-card information through a general support form.

## Marketing Communications

If you subscribe to emails, SMS messages, offers, or other marketing communications, we may use your contact information to send those communications where permitted by law.

You may opt out of marketing communications by using the unsubscribe instructions in the message or by contacting us. Even if you opt out of marketing messages, we may still send transactional, service, support, safety, policy, or administrative messages.

## Children

The website is not intended for children to submit personal information directly. Some Ovkara content may help parents or guardians evaluate youth riding decisions, but parents or guardians should handle youth fit, safety, purchase, support, warranty, and return communications.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no website, online service, transmission method, or storage system is completely secure.

You are responsible for using the website safely and for keeping any account credentials confidential if account features are added.

## Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide services, process orders, support warranty and returns, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent fraud, and protect safety.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the reason it was collected, operational needs, legal requirements, and dispute or warranty considerations.

## Your Rights And Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or opt-out of certain processing activities.

To make a privacy request, contact us through the support channel listed below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We may deny or limit requests where permitted by law, including when information is needed for security, legal compliance, warranty, dispute, tax, accounting, or business-record purposes.

## United States State Privacy Rights

Residents of certain U.S. states may have additional privacy rights under applicable state privacy laws. These rights may include access, deletion, correction, portability, opt-out of targeted advertising, opt-out of sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to appeal certain decisions.

Ovkara's exact state-law disclosures should be updated when the final ecommerce, advertising, analytics, and customer-data stack is confirmed.

## International Users

Ovkara is currently focused on the United States market. If you access the website from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other locations where we or our service providers operate.

Those locations may have data-protection laws that differ from the laws in your jurisdiction.

## Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle personal information, please contact us first so we can review and respond. Depending on your location, you may also have the right to contact a privacy regulator or other authority.
