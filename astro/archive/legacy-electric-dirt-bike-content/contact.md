# Contact Support

Choose the request type and send the details needed for a useful first reply. Keep sensitive financial, payment-card, and identity information out of general support messages.

<div class="support-brief">
  <article><span>Buying Help</span><p>Share rider size, experience, terrain, budget, and the models you are comparing.</p></article>
  <article><span>Order Help</span><p>Send order number, delivery ZIP, tracking status, and the question.</p></article>
  <article><span>Technical Help</span><p>Include model, serial number, symptom, photos/video when safe, and maintenance history.</p></article>
  <article><span>Dealer Inquiry</span><p>Include company, location, channel, expected volume, territory, and models of interest.</p></article>
</div>

<div class="support-detail-grid">
  <section class="support-card">
    <h2>For Product Selection</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Adult, teen, or child rider.</li>
      <li>Height, inseam, approximate weight, and experience.</li>
      <li>Primary terrain and riding location.</li>
      <li>Performance, range, charging, transport, and budget priorities.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="support-card support-card--dark">
    <h2>For Service, Parts, Or Warranty</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Model, SKU, and serial number.</li>
      <li>Order information or proof of purchase.</li>
      <li>Symptoms, warning indicators, and timeline.</li>
      <li>Photos/video when safe, plus modification and maintenance history.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

<div class="support-detail-grid">
  <section class="support-card support-card--dark">
    <h2>Privacy Boundary</h2>
    <p>Use the general form only for non-sensitive information. Do not upload government IDs, complete payment-card information, or unrelated personal data.</p>
    <p>Contact details, order details, serial numbers, photos, and video should be used only to route and resolve the support request.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="support-card">
    <h2>Response Expectations</h2>
    <p>Every support request should receive a human review before a buyer is told that a model, delivery route, claim, return, or legal-use path is approved.</p>
    <p>This channel is not an emergency service. For immediate danger, fire, injury, or another emergency, contact local emergency services.</p>
  </section>
</div>
