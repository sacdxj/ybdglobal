# K2-60-MY-1417 60V Electric Dirt Bike

The K2-60-MY-1417 is the 60V step-up model in the 14/17 K2 frame family. It shares the same
1600 mm overall length and 1150 mm wheelbase as the 48V MY model, while using a 60V 22.5Ah
domestic ternary lithium battery and a 1500W rated mid-mounted motor.

## FAQ

### Is 60V better than 48V?

Not automatically. The 60V model increases rated and maximum motor output, but fit, terrain,
charging time, support, price, and rider experience still decide whether it is the better bike.

### Why does the page show both 22.5Ah and 25Ah?

The Excel source workbook lists `60V22.5AH` in the battery-type row and `25AH` in the
battery-capacity row. This page follows the source table instead of choosing one value.
