# Electric Dirt Bike Repair & Service

Start with the exact model, serial number, symptom, visible condition, and warranty status. Support can then route the issue by model and risk level.

<div class="support-brief">
  <article><span>Stop Use</span><p>Stop riding if brakes, battery, wiring, frame, heat, smoke, odor, or throttle behavior feels unsafe.</p></article>
  <article><span>Identify Model</span><p>Model, serial number, and order details are needed before parts or repair guidance.</p></article>
  <article><span>Document Issue</span><p>Photos, video when safe, warning codes, and timeline help support triage quickly.</p></article>
  <article><span>No Battery DIY</span><p>Do not open, rebuild, bypass, or modify high-voltage components.</p></article>
</div>

<div class="support-detail-grid">
  <section class="support-card support-card--dark">
    <h2>Contact Support Immediately When</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Battery case is damaged, hot, smoking, sparking, swollen, or smells unusual.</li>
      <li>Brake function, steering, wheels, frame, or suspension feels unsafe.</li>
      <li>Throttle, controller, wiring, or display behavior is uncontrolled or inconsistent.</li>
      <li>The bike was crashed, submerged, transported roughly, or modified.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="support-card">
    <h2>Send These Details</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Model and serial number.</li>
      <li>Order or proof of purchase.</li>
      <li>Current location and whether the bike can be transported safely.</li>
      <li>Issue description, timing, warning codes, photos/video, maintenance, impact, water exposure, or modifications.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

<div class="support-detail-grid">
  <section class="support-card">
    <h2>Allowed First Step</h2>
    <p>Use only non-invasive checks approved for the exact model. Do not open batteries, bypass safety systems, alter wiring, or defeat controller limits.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="support-card support-card--dark">

  </section>
</div>
