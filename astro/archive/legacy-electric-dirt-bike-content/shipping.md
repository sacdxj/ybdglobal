# Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Shipping Policy explains how Ovkara may process, ship, deliver, track, and support electric dirt bike orders.

Electric dirt bikes are large, high-value products. Delivery timing, cost, handling requirements, carrier method, and availability may vary by model, shipping address, warehouse, inventory status, carrier capacity, weather, holidays, and other operational conditions.

Please review this policy before placing an order.

## Shipping Area

Ovkara currently ships eligible products within the contiguous United States through standard checkout.

Standard checkout may not support delivery to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, U.S. territories, APO/FPO/DPO addresses, PO boxes, remote islands, international addresses, or certain remote or restricted delivery areas unless Ovkara support approves a separate written arrangement.

If you are unsure whether we can ship to your location, contact support before ordering.

## Shipping Cost

Shipping costs, if any, will be shown on the product page, quote, cart, checkout, or order confirmation when available.

Some eligible electric dirt bike orders may include free shipping when the product page or checkout states that free shipping applies.

Additional fees may apply for remote areas, special handling, liftgate service, residential freight delivery, missed appointments, storage, re-delivery, address correction, re-routing, refused delivery, return freight, or order changes after fulfillment begins.

## Shipping From

Orders may ship from an Ovkara warehouse, supplier warehouse, fulfillment partner, or other approved shipping location depending on model, inventory, and destination.

Items in the same order may ship separately. Accessories, parts, documents, and electric dirt bikes may use different carriers or delivery methods.

## Order Processing Time

Most in-stock orders are processed within 1-3 business days after payment, order review, and address verification.

Processing time may be longer for preorders, backorders, payment review, fraud review, address verification, warehouse delays, custom handling, documentation review, holidays, high-volume periods, or freight scheduling.

Business days do not include weekends or U.S. holidays.

## Transit Time

After carrier pickup, most eligible shipments commonly arrive within 2-9 business days depending on destination, carrier capacity, route, weather, holidays, freight appointment scheduling, and delivery exceptions.

Transit estimates are not guaranteed unless a specific guaranteed delivery service is stated in writing.

Do not schedule first rides, events, travel, service appointments, installation work, or time-sensitive plans until your order has been delivered, inspected, assembled where required, and confirmed to be in usable condition.

## Freight Delivery

Some electric dirt bike orders may ship by truck freight, LTL freight, or another large-item delivery method.

Freight delivery may require a valid phone number, delivery appointment, adult signature, curbside receiving, unloading plan, or delivery access that can accommodate a large shipment.

Delivery may be curbside or threshold only unless a different service is specifically approved in writing. Drivers may not unpack, assemble, inspect, move the product inside, remove packaging, or provide technical service.

## Address Changes

Please review your shipping address, phone number, product selection, and order details carefully before placing an order.

If you need to change an address or phone number, contact support as soon as possible. Address changes are not guaranteed once order processing, labeling, fulfillment, or carrier pickup has begun.

Address changes, re-routes, storage, re-delivery, or corrections may create additional carrier or handling fees.

## Order Cancellations Before Shipment

Orders can usually be canceled before fulfillment begins.

Once an order has been packed, labeled, scheduled, loaded, or handed to the carrier, cancellation is not guaranteed. Shipping, return freight, re-route, storage, carrier, payment-processing, or restocking fees may apply where permitted by law.

## Tracking

When tracking is available, tracking information may be sent by email, SMS, customer account, or another order-status method.

Tracking may take time to update after a label is created. A tracking number does not always mean the carrier has picked up the shipment.

For freight shipments, the carrier may contact you to schedule delivery. Please respond promptly to delivery calls, texts, emails, or appointment requests.

## Delivery Inspection

Inspect the package and product before assembly, charging, or riding.

When the shipment arrives:

- Check the carton and packaging before accepting delivery where possible.
- Photograph all sides of the carton and any visible damage.
- Note visible damage with the carrier where possible.
- Confirm model, package count, labels, and included items.
- Keep all packaging until the product is inspected and confirmed.
- Photograph any concealed damage before assembly.
- Do not charge, assemble, ride, modify, or dispose of packaging if damage is visible or suspected.

## Damaged, Missing, Or Incorrect Shipments

Visible or concealed shipping damage, missing items, or incorrect items should be reported to support as soon as possible and within 48 hours of delivery.

Your report should include:

- Order number.
- Delivery ZIP code.
- Tracking number.
- Product model and serial number if available.
- Photos of the carton, labels, packaging, product, and damage.
- Description of the issue.

Ovkara may need photos, labels, packaging, carrier documentation, inspection details, and customer cooperation to review a delivery issue or file a carrier claim.

## Lost, Delayed, Or Failed Delivery

Carrier delays can happen because of weather, holidays, route capacity, missed appointments, inaccessible delivery locations, address errors, freight scheduling, customs or documentation issues where applicable, or other carrier conditions.

If tracking stalls, delivery fails, or a shipment appears lost, contact support with the order number, model, tracking number, delivery ZIP code, phone number, and current status.

Carrier investigations may require additional time.

## Refused Delivery

Do not refuse delivery unless support or the carrier specifically instructs you to do so.

Refused delivery may result in return freight, storage, re-delivery, re-route, inspection, or restocking fees where permitted by law.

If the package appears damaged, photograph the condition and contact support for instructions.

## Assembly And First Ride

Shipping and delivery do not replace final assembly, inspection, charging review, brake checks, fastener checks, tire-pressure checks, battery review, protective gear, adult supervision for youth riders, or safe first-ride preparation.

Follow the exact product manual and support instructions for your model.

## Risk Of Loss

Risk of loss and responsibility for a shipment may transfer according to the applicable shipping terms, carrier rules, payment terms, and law.

Ovkara will work with customers and carriers to review eligible shipping issues, but customers must provide accurate delivery information, respond to carrier scheduling, inspect delivery promptly, retain packaging, and report issues within the required time window.
