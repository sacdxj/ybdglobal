# Refund Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how returns, refunds, cancellations, damaged deliveries, warranty reviews, and related support requests may be handled by Ovkara.

Please read this policy carefully before placing an order. Electric dirt bikes are large, high-value products, and return eligibility may depend on product condition, packaging, use, authorization, shipping status, inspection results, and applicable law.

## Return Window

Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery.

To be eligible for a return, the product must generally be unused, clean, undamaged, unmodified, complete, and in resalable condition. The product must also include the original packaging, manuals, charger, keys, tools, accessories, and all included parts.

All returns must be authorized by Ovkara support before anything is shipped back. Items returned without authorization may be refused, delayed, denied, or subject to additional costs where permitted by law.

## How To Start A Return

To request a return, contact support through:

[Contact Support](/support/contact/)

Your return request should include:

- Order number or proof of purchase.
- Full name, email address, phone number, and delivery ZIP code.
- Product model, SKU, color, and serial number if available.
- Reason for return.
- Photos of the product, packaging, accessories, labels, and condition.
- Confirmation that the product is unused, undamaged, unmodified, and complete.

Ovkara support will review the request and, if approved, provide return instructions. Do not ship the product back before receiving approved instructions.

## Return Shipping

For buyer's-remorse returns, incorrect model choice, refusal unrelated to product defect, or other non-defect returns, the customer is generally responsible for return shipping costs, packaging costs, insurance, freight handling, and any applicable restocking, inspection, cleaning, repair, or re-delivery fees where permitted by law.

For approved warranty, damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing-item cases, support will provide case-specific instructions.

Original shipping charges, return shipping charges, re-route fees, storage fees, refused-delivery fees, missed appointment fees, liftgate fees, remote-area fees, and other carrier or fulfillment charges may be non-refundable unless required by law or approved by Ovkara in writing.

## Packaging Requirements

Returned products must be packed securely for parcel or freight handling. Original packaging should be used whenever possible.

Customers are responsible for damage caused by inadequate return packaging unless support approves a different process in writing.

Do not ship batteries, chargers, electrical parts, or full bikes in unsafe, damaged, wet, altered, or non-compliant packaging.

## Inspection And Refund Approval

Returned products are inspected after arrival. A refund is not approved until the product is received and inspected.

Ovkara may deny a refund or deduct costs where permitted by law if the returned product is:

- Used, ridden, damaged, dirty, altered, modified, incomplete, or unsafe.
- Missing original packaging, manuals, charger, keys, tools, accessories, or included parts.
- Returned without authorization.
- Returned after the approved return window.
- Damaged during return shipping because of inadequate packaging.
- Different from the product originally shipped.

## Refund Timing

If a refund is approved after inspection, the refund will be issued to the original payment method unless another method is required by law or approved by Ovkara.

Payment processors, banks, card issuers, and financing providers may require additional time to post the refund. Ovkara does not control third-party processing times.

If the original purchase used financing, installment payments, promotional credit, or another third-party payment provider, the refund may be subject to that provider's terms, timing, fees, and adjustment process.

## Order Cancellations

Orders can usually be canceled before fulfillment begins.

After an order has been packed, labeled, handed to a carrier, scheduled for freight pickup, or otherwise entered fulfillment, cancellation is not guaranteed. Shipping, return, re-route, storage, carrier, payment-processing, or restocking fees may apply where permitted by law.

If you need to cancel an order, contact support as soon as possible with your order number.

## Damaged, Incorrect, Or Missing Items

Inspect the package and product before assembly, charging, or riding.

If your order arrives visibly damaged, appears to have concealed damage, is missing items, or contains the wrong item, contact support as soon as possible and within 48 hours of delivery.

Your request should include:

- Order number.
- Product model and serial number if available.
- Photos of all sides of the carton.
- Photos of shipping labels.
- Photos or video of the damage, missing item, or incorrect item.
- Description of the issue.

Do not charge, assemble, ride, modify, dispose of packaging, or ship anything back until support provides instructions.

## Non-Returnable Or Restricted Items

The following items may be non-returnable or restricted unless support approves an exception in writing:

- Used, ridden, damaged, dirty, modified, altered, or incomplete electric dirt bikes.
- Batteries, chargers, controllers, motors, wiring, electrical parts, and opened parts.
- Custom orders, special orders, final-sale items, closeout items, discounted items marked final sale, or items not purchased directly from Ovkara or an approved channel.
- Used safety gear, apparel, accessories, consumables, wear items, and parts that have been installed.
- Products returned without authorization or outside the return window.

## Exchanges

Exchanges depend on product condition, return eligibility, inventory, shipping availability, price differences, and support approval.

Do not assume that an exchange is available until support confirms it in writing.

## Warranty Review

Ovkara provides a 12-month limited warranty for eligible electric dirt bikes purchased directly from Ovkara or an approved Ovkara sales channel. The warranty begins on the delivery date and applies to the original purchaser in the United States.

Warranty coverage may apply to eligible manufacturing defects. Coverage depends on model, component, use case, evidence, product condition, maintenance, modifications, and the final warranty decision.

Warranty review may require:

- Order number or proof of purchase.
- Product model, SKU, color, and serial number.
- Issue description and timeline.
- Photos or video when safe.
- Error codes or display messages if available.
- Charging history, storage history, maintenance history, and riding context.
- Details of crashes, water exposure, transport damage, repairs, or modifications.

## Warranty Exclusions

The limited warranty does not cover normal wear, crash damage, misuse, neglect, improper assembly, improper charging, water exposure, overheating, commercial use, competition use, unauthorized repair, aftermarket modification, incompatible parts, cosmetic damage, missing documentation, or damage caused by shipping, transport, storage, or unsafe use unless otherwise required by law.

Unauthorized modifications may affect safety, compatibility, return eligibility, and warranty review.

## Safety Issues

If a product may be unsafe, stop use immediately and contact support.

Do not continue riding, charging, transporting, modifying, or troubleshooting a product when there may be a battery, brake, wiring, controller, throttle, frame, wheel, heat, smoke, odor, fire, or uncontrolled-behavior risk.
