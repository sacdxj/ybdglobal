# Electric Dirt Bike Charging Guide

Charging is a compatibility and safety workflow. Use only the charger approved for the exact
model and battery. Connector shape alone does not prove compatibility.

## Charging Compatibility Checklist

| Check | What must match |
|---|---|
| Bike model | Exact model and configuration. |
| Battery | Battery label, voltage, capacity, chemistry, and part number where available. |
| Charger | Approved charger part number and output rating. |
| Connector | Connector type plus any communication or lock requirements. |
| Manual | Current manual revision and charging instructions. |
| Condition | No visible damage, abnormal heat, swelling, moisture issue, warning, or cable damage. |

Contact support before replacing a charger or charging a battery with unclear labels.

## Before You Plug In

1. Confirm the bike, battery, and charger records match.
2. Inspect the charger, cable, connector, battery area, and charging port.
3. Choose a charging location that follows the manual.
4. Check temperature, moisture, ventilation, and supervision requirements.
5. Stop and contact support if anything looks damaged or abnormal.

## Charging Time

Charging time depends on battery state, capacity, charger output, temperature, battery
management, and product guidance. Use the exact model page and manual for model-specific time.

Charging time is best compared when the starting state of charge, charger output, temperature,
battery condition, and method are documented.

## Storage and Infrequent Use

Do not publish one universal storage percentage for every battery. Storage guidance must come
from the exact model manual or approved battery documentation.

[Read Battery Life & Care](/buying-guides/battery-life/)

## Replacement Chargers

Support should ask for:

- Model and serial number.
- Battery and charger label photos.
- Connector photos.
- Description of damage, warning lights, heat, moisture, or unusual behavior.
- Order or purchase information where relevant.

Do not recommend third-party, higher-output, modified, or improvised chargers without written
product approval.

## When to Stop and Contact Support

Stop charging and contact support if the battery, charger, cable, connector, or bike shows
damage, unusual heat, smell, warning indicators, water exposure beyond approved conditions, or
behavior not covered by the manual.

## FAQ

### Can I use another charger if the plug fits?

No. Plug fit does not prove electrical or battery-system compatibility.

### How long does charging take?

Use approved model-specific data. Battery state, charger output, temperature, and battery
management all affect time.

### Can I charge immediately after riding?

Follow the manual's cooldown and temperature guidance for the exact battery.
