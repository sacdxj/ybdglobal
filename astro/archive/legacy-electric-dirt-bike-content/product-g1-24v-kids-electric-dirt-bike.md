# G1 24V Kids Electric Dirt Bike

The G1 is the current entry-level kids product candidate in the catalog. It uses a 24V 1.5Ah
lead-acid battery, a 180W wheel-hub motor, 10-inch tires, a 420 mm seat height, and a listed top
speed of 9.94 mph.

This page should be written for parents first. The key question is not whether a child matches a
keyword age, but whether the rider fits this exact bike, can manage the controls, and will ride
only in an appropriate supervised area.

## Best For

- Parents comparing a small 24V electric dirt bike for supervised private-property riding.
- Riders who fit a 420 mm seat height and can manage an 18.5 kg curb weight.
- Lower-speed learning environments where space, surface, supervision, and protective gear are
  controlled.
- Buyers who want a compact product below the 48V K2 models.

## Not the Best Fit For

- Teens or larger riders who exceed the documented fit or load guidance.
- Public-road, sidewalk, or unsupervised riding.
- Buyers expecting adult electric dirt bike speed, suspension, or range.
- Families who need a lithium battery system rather than lead-acid.

## Parent Fit and Readiness

Before adding the G1 to a youth collection, confirm:

- Rider height and inseam against the 420 mm seat height.
- Ability to balance, steer, stop, and follow instructions.
- Brake reach and throttle control.
- Whether the rider can manage the 18.5 kg curb weight.
- Riding area boundaries, surface, supervision, and protective equipment.

Age labels should remain secondary to measurements and behavior.

## Battery and Charging

The G1 uses a 24V 1.5Ah lead-acid battery and a 1.5A lead-acid charger. Charging time is listed
as 4-5 hours. Parents should control charging, storage, replacement-battery questions, and
inspection.

## Chassis and Components

The source table lists an iron frame, powder coating, 10-inch tires, no front suspension, no
rear suspension, disc brake, LCD display, adaptive headlight, and a four-layer elastic leather
saddle with high-elasticity sponge.

Because suspension is listed as none, this bike should be positioned for controlled smooth
surfaces unless the product manual approves other terrain.
