# Electric Dirt Bike Financing

Financing can divide a purchase into payments, but it should only happen after the right bike, total cost, and return path are clear.

<div class="support-brief">
  <article><span>Approval Varies</span><p>Eligibility depends on the provider, applicant, jurisdiction, product, and order value.</p></article>
  <article><span>Total Cost Matters</span><p>Compare monthly payment with cash price, APR, fees, tax, shipping, gear, and maintenance.</p></article>
  <article><span>Not A Discount</span><p>Financing should not be described as free unless provider terms support that exact claim.</p></article>
  <article><span>Refund Rules</span><p>Returns and cancellations must follow both store policy and provider process.</p></article>
</div>

<div class="support-detail-grid">
  <section class="support-card">
    <h2>Before Applying</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Total purchase amount and delivery cost.</li>
      <li>Down payment, number of payments, and payment dates.</li>
      <li>APR, fees, late-payment terms, and total financing cost.</li>
      <li>Credit implications and provider disclosures.</li>
      <li>How returns, cancellations, and refunds are handled.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

<div class="support-faq-grid">
  <details><summary>Does everyone qualify?</summary><p>No approval should be implied. Eligibility depends on the provider's process and terms.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Does financing affect credit?</summary><p>Use the provider's exact disclosure before applying.</p></details>
  <details><summary>What happens if I return the bike?</summary><p>Follow both the store return policy and the provider refund process.</p></details>
  <details><summary>Is a monthly payment the total cost?</summary><p>No. Review payments, APR, fees, down payment, taxes, shipping, gear, and maintenance.</p></details>
</div>
