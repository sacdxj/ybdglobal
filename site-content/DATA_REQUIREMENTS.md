# Data Requirements

## Product catalog

- SKU/model ID
- product family and connector/port configuration
- supported protocol/version
- power, bandwidth, resolution and refresh data
- length, material, jacket, shell, housing and color
- test method and report reference
- product image and packaging image

## Commercial

- MOQ by product/customization
- sample cost and timing
- production lead time
- quantity pricing process
- tooling/NRE policy
- payment and shipping terms

## Company and factory

- legal company name and address
- approved brand and domain
- production location and role
- equipment and process evidence
- quality-control workflow
- capacity statement with source

## Compliance and policies

- certificates mapped to specific models/markets
- warranty and returns
- shipping and damage process
- privacy/form processors
- RFQ email or CRM endpoint

Until supplied, active pages remain `private-review` and use confirmation language.
