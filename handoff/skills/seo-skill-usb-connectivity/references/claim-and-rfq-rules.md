# Claim and RFQ Rules

## Claims requiring source evidence

Require a current SKU sheet, test report, certificate, policy or signed company statement before publishing:

- protocol/version compliance;
- wattage, current and voltage;
- bandwidth, resolution and refresh rate;
- cable length limits and long-distance performance;
- safety and market certifications;
- MOQ, sample cost, price and lead time;
- production capacity, factory size, years in business and customer claims;
- warranty, returns, logistics and after-sales promises.

Use wording such as “project option,” “confirm by selected SKU,” or “evidence required before publication” when a real option may exist but proof is missing.

## RFQ minimum fields

- product family and connector/port combination;
- required power, protocol, bandwidth or display output;
- length, jacket, housing, color and materials;
- quantity and forecast;
- logo, packaging, barcode and private-label requirements;
- destination country and certification market;
- sample need and target delivery date;
- company, buyer name and contact method.

## CTA order

1. Get a Quote
2. Configure & Quote
3. Request a Sample
4. Discuss Private Label
5. Request Evidence Pack

Do not default to Buy Now unless the business explicitly adds online stock, pricing, payment and fulfillment operations.
