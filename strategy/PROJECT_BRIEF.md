# USB Connectivity & Charging B2B Website Brief

## Positioning

This is a Google-to-RFQ B2B acquisition website for wholesalers, distributors, importers, electronics brands, private-label programs and ecommerce retailers. It is not a consumer checkout store.

Primary scope:

- USB, USB-C and USB4 charging/data cables
- HDMI 2.0, HDMI 2.1 and fiber/AOC HDMI cables
- DisplayPort 1.4, DisplayPort 2.1 and high-refresh cable requirements
- USB-C hubs and laptop docking stations
- USB-C, PD and GaN chargers

## Conversion model

1. Get a Quote
2. Configure & Quote
3. Request a Sample
4. Discuss Private Label
5. Request Evidence Pack

## Evidence boundary

Protocol, wattage, bandwidth, resolution, refresh rate, length performance, certification, MOQ, price, lead time, capacity and warranty require current source evidence. Missing values remain configuration-dependent and private-review.
