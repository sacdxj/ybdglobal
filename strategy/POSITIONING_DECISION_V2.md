# USB Connectivity Project Positioning Decision V2

Status: approved working decision for keyword cleaning and page planning. Public brand identity remains unapproved.

## Business model

Build a B2B Google acquisition and RFQ qualification system. The primary conversion is a specification-complete inquiry, not retail checkout.

## Market position

> A B2B connectivity and charging accessories supplier/manufacturer platform for USB cables, HDMI cables, DisplayPort cables, USB-C hubs, docking stations and chargers.

The site must connect product discovery, specification selection, OEM/private-label requirements, evidence review and sales follow-up.

## Primary buyers

1. Electronics wholesalers
2. Importers and distributors
3. Consumer-electronics and computer-accessories brands
4. Amazon and marketplace private-label sellers
5. Retail channel buyers

Consumer end users may create informational traffic, but they are not the core conversion audience.

## Phase-one product boundary

### Include

- USB and USB-C charging/data cables
- USB4 cables
- HDMI 2.0/2.1 and copper/fiber/AOC HDMI cables
- DisplayPort 1.4/2.1 and high-refresh display cables
- USB-C hubs and laptop docking stations
- USB-C, PD and GaN wall chargers/power adapters

### Exclude from the phase-one sitemap

- Lightning-only, Micro USB and Mini USB product ranges unless supported by the final catalog
- Consumer replacement and troubleshooting queries
- Retailer/store navigation and “near me” queries
- Brand-specific accessory pages unless a later comparison/compatibility policy approves them
- Repair parts, car battery, printer, Ethernet, coaxial, VGA, DVI, SATA and unrelated cable categories
- General electronics liquidation, pallets and broken-electronics wholesale
- Product categories without a plausible supported SKU path

## SEO architecture

Derive pages from cleaned keyword clusters across four intent classes:

1. `Product`: category and product-type demand
2. `Specification`: power, bandwidth, protocol, resolution, refresh, length, construction and port mix
3. `Procurement`: manufacturer/supplier/factory, wholesale/bulk, OEM/ODM/custom/private label
4. `Buyer solution`: wholesaler, importer/distributor, electronics brand, Amazon seller and retailer

Informational keywords may support these commercial clusters through resource pages.

## Conversion hierarchy

1. Get a Quote
2. Configure & Quote
3. Request a Sample
4. Discuss Private Label
5. Request Evidence Pack

## Page approval rules

A page may enter the recommended sitemap only when:

- the cluster contains a coherent search task;
- the business can plausibly serve the query;
- the page has a distinct role from its parent and sibling pages;
- a product, buyer or educational path can support it;
- the CTA matches the searcher's stage;
- evidence requirements are explicit.

Search volume alone never creates a page. Manually supplied seed terms remain visible even when the source workbook has no measured row, but are labeled as manual inputs.
