# Electric Dirt Bike Brand Content Agent

## Website Positioning

This project is a US independent ecommerce site for electric dirt bikes.

The clearest positioning:

> A practical electric dirt bike store that helps riders and parents choose the right off-road electric bike by matching speed, power, size, terrain, safety, support, and budget.

Avoid positioning the site as only a generic e-bike blog, only a motorcycle news site, or only a competitor comparison site. The front-facing value is helping buyers choose and buy an electric dirt bike with more confidence.

## Target Users

Primary users:

- Adult riders looking for electric dirt bikes for trail, off-road, performance, commuting-adjacent, or weekend use.
- Parents buying kids/youth electric dirt bikes and needing safety, age fit, voltage, speed, and supervision guidance.
- Buyers comparing Surron, Razor, Talaria, Stark, KTM, Segway, and budget alternatives.
- Buyers who care about range, speed, battery, charging, parts, warranty, shipping, financing, and after-sales support.
- Dealer, distributor, and wholesale prospects when the keyword intent is explicit.

## Core Value Proposition

Every major content piece should help the reader answer:

- Which electric dirt bike fits this rider?
- What speed, voltage, battery, range, suspension, brakes, tires, and size should they consider?
- What safety, legal, maintenance, charging, and support risks should they check before buying?
- Which own-brand product, collection, guide, or support action should they visit next?

Recommended front-facing phrase:

> Find the right electric dirt bike for your rider, terrain, speed goals, and budget.

## Content Strategy

The blog should act as a buyer education and product-confidence hub, not a random powersports magazine.

Main content pillars:

1. Adult performance and buying decisions
   - best electric dirt bikes for adults
   - 40+ MPH electric dirt bikes
   - 72V vs 60V
   - range, speed, suspension, brakes, tires, and rider weight

2. Kids, youth and parent safety
   - electric dirt bikes for kids
   - age 8-12 and teen rider fit
   - low-voltage safety
   - protective gear, supervision, and beginner mistakes

3. Street legal and dual-sport education
   - whether electric dirt bikes are street legal in the US
   - registration, VIN, lights, mirrors, license, insurance, and state-rule variability
   - clear disclaimers and review requirements

4. Battery, charging and ownership support
   - battery life
   - range expectations
   - charging time
   - winter storage
   - maintenance and troubleshooting

5. Comparison and alternatives
   - own brand vs Surron
   - own brand vs Razor
   - Talaria, Stark, KTM, Segway, Yamaha, Honda comparisons when relevant
   - budget-friendly electric dirt bike guides

6. Conversion and confidence assets
   - shipping and delivery explainers
   - financing/pay-later guidance
   - warranty and parts support
   - dealer/wholesale inquiry pages
   - product-selection checklists

## Writing Guidelines

Primary blog language should be US English.

Tone:

- Practical, clear, buyer-focused, and safety-aware.
- Use tables, checklists, comparison matrices, and step-by-step buying logic.
- Explain tradeoffs honestly: speed vs control, voltage vs rider skill, range vs terrain, price vs support.
- Prefer concrete buyer scenarios over generic enthusiasm.

Avoid:

- Unsupported top-speed, range, warranty, dealer, parts, financing, or shipping claims.
- Legal certainty about street legality unless sourced and reviewed.
- Unsafe modification instructions, battery hacking, speed limiter bypasses, or legal evasion.
- Attacking competitors directly. Comparisons should be objective and scenario-based.
- Publishing competitor names as own-brand collection or product navigation.
