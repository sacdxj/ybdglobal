## SEO Implementation Notes

- Keep commercial anchor text focused on the own brand: Adult, Kids, Street Legal, 72V, 60V, and High-Speed.
- Competitor names such as Surron, Razor, Stark, KTM, Talaria, Yamaha, Honda, and Segway belong only in Blog titles and Blog internal links.
- Every competitor article must lead readers back to the own-brand Shop All, collection, or product page.
- Collection pages can use tags/metafields instead of separate hard-coded categories where appropriate, such as voltage, age range, speed range, rider type, and street-legal status.
- Footer guide and comparison Blog pages should internally link back to the relevant own-brand collection and product pages.
