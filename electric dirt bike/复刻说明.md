# Electric Dirt Bike Replication Notes

This is the Electric Dirt Bike independent-store SEO project. It follows the same operating model as the Zhutova project, but the business boundary, keyword rules, site structure, article brief rules, and content pillars are now specific to a US electric dirt bike ecommerce site.

## Preserved Functions

- Keyword cleaning and classification.
- Hub / Pillar / Topic / Cluster architecture.
- Build now / Build later / Parent-FAQ / Review / Reject production decisions.
- Semantic QA workbook requirements.
- SERP validation workbook requirements.
- Streamlit SEO dashboard.
- Article brief exporter.
- Local Codex skill.
- Blog and social content reuse logic.

## Current Positioning

- Main Menu: Shop All, Adult Dirt Bikes, Kids & Youth, Street Legal, Buying Guides.
- Footer Menu: Customer Support, Buying Guides, Compare & Alternatives Blog.
- Core SEO intent: adult performance, kids/youth, low-voltage safety, street legal, 72V/60V, speed, Surron/Razor/Talaria alternatives, budget buying, battery/range, parts, maintenance, shipping, financing, dealer and wholesale.
- Competitor names such as Surron and Razor are restricted to Blog comparison content and must not appear as own-brand collection pillars or product navigation.

## Keep-Function Rule

Only change the product boundary, keyword judgment, page content, copy rules, and output filenames.

Do not delete fields, do not delete sheets, do not change production decision field names, and do not remove semantic QA or SERP evidence sheet requirements.

Old Zhutova project content is method reference only. It is not a publishable content or data source for Electric Dirt Bike.
