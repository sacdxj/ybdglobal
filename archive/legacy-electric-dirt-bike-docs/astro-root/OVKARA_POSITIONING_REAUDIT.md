# Ovkara Positioning Re-Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-01

## Positioning Standard

Ovkara should read as:

```text
An American electric dirt bike decision brand.
```

Not:

```text
A keyword ecommerce catalog.
A lowest-price marketplace.
A thin affiliate comparison site.
A product-first powersports store.
```

The core promise remains:

```text
Ovkara helps riders choose electric dirt bikes with confidence.
```

## Current Verdict

The site now fits the Decision Brand positioning at the brand, navigation, homepage, and private
review gate level.

It is still not ready for public launch because product proof, policy proof, legal/safety review,
final visual identity, trademark clearance, support workflow, and analytics setup are not complete.

## Passed Positioning Checks

- Global brand is now `Ovkara`, not the working category label.
- Canonical domain is now `https://ovkara.com`.
- Header uses `Decision Guides` and `Compare Models` before product browsing.
- Homepage primary action is `Find Your Bike`.
- Decision Engine routes buyers by rider, use case, concern, and buyer stage.
- Product browsing appears after decision logic.
- Footer brand copy says confidence before checkout.
- Support and policy pages remain conservative instead of overpromising.
- Street-legal pages avoid unsupported legality claims.
- Youth content remains parent-led and fit/safety aware.
- Competitor pages are comparison tools, not forced sales pages.
- Private review protection remains active with `noindex, nofollow` and `robots.txt` disallowing
  all crawling.

## Issues Found In This Re-Audit

### 1. Shop-First Language

Problem:

Several public links and page labels still used:

```text
Shop All
Shop Electric Dirt Bikes
Shop Our Bikes
Shop by Current Price
```

Why it conflicted:

Those labels make Ovkara feel like a store before it feels like a Guide / Navigator.

Action taken:

Updated visible language toward:

```text
Compare Models
Compare Ovkara Models
Build a Decision Shortlist
Compare by Current Price
```

### 2. Internal Brand Language

Problem:

Public-facing content still used `own-brand`, which is an internal business term.

Why it conflicted:

Buyers do not buy "own-brand candidates." They compare Ovkara models and decision paths.

Action taken:

Replaced public `own-brand` language with `Ovkara` language across content and components.

### 3. Legacy Audit Drift

Problem:

The original decision-brand audit still described the site as using the working name.

Why it conflicted:

After `Ovkara.com` was purchased, the site needed a new status layer.

Action taken:

This re-audit supersedes the original launch-positioning findings for brand/domain status.

## Remaining Non-Negotiable Blockers

The site should not be public-indexed until these are complete:

- Formal trademark / common-law / social-handle / negative-meaning clearance for `Ovkara`.
- Final logo and visual identity system.
- Real company, support email, support form endpoint, phone/address if used, and response hours.
- Approved product prices, inventory, images, manuals, fit guidance, and SKU data.
- Approved shipping, warranty, returns, financing, parts, repair, and battery safety policies.
- Youth safety review.
- Street-legal legal review.
- Analytics, event tracking, consent/privacy workflow, and conversion reporting.

## Current Gate

```text
Positioning fit: Pass for private review
Public launch: Blocked
Next step: Ovkara Brand Identity V1 + formal clearance + proof/policy completion
```
