# Ovkara Visual Identity V1

## Purpose

This document defines the first visual identity gate for Ovkara.

Ovkara is not positioned as a racing-only bike brand. It is an electric dirt bike decision brand
that helps riders choose with confidence. The visual system must therefore balance off-road
energy with clarity, guidance, and support.

## Identity Status

```text
Current logo/favicon: Provisional
Current website palette: Usable for private review
Public launch identity: Not approved
```

The current logo and favicon can be used as internal review assets, but they should not be
treated as final production identity until the stress tests below pass.

## Brand Role

| Layer | Visual implication |
|---|---|
| Decision brand | Clear hierarchy, readable UI, proof-first layouts. |
| Guide / Navigator | Directional cues, route language, comparison structure, support confidence. |
| Electric off-road | Motion, terrain, rugged contrast, product realism. |
| Rider-first support | Warm utility, protective tone, no reckless performance styling. |

## Typography Direction

| Use | Direction |
|---|---|
| UI/body | Inter or system sans for clarity and speed. |
| Wordmark | Custom or modified sans with clear `K`, open `a`, and non-Vokera silhouette. |
| Hero headlines | Heavy, compact, confident; no negative letter spacing in final CSS. |
| Product/spec text | Dense, readable, decision-first. |

## Logo Requirements

The final logo must pass all tests:

| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Wordmark clarity | `Ovkara` reads correctly at desktop nav, mobile nav, invoice/email, and product detail sizes. |
| K/C distinction | The `K` cannot be mistaken for `C`; avoid any mark that creates `Ovcara`. |
| Vokera separation | The silhouette must not read like `Vokera`, `Vokèra`, or `Ovkera`. |
| Favicon | Works at 16px, 32px, 64px without losing category signal. |
| Social avatar | Works as a circle-cropped YouTube/Instagram/TikTok/X avatar. |
| Product decal | Works on bike frame, battery label, charger label, helmet, glove, and apparel. |
| Monochrome | Works in black, white, and one-color laser/emboss/decal use. |
| Motion | Can animate into Decision Engine or video bumper without looking like a pure racing brand. |

## Image Direction

Approved visual content should show:

- Real or realistic electric dirt bikes.
- Rider-fit context, terrain, battery/charging, support, parts, and ownership decisions.
- Product details that help buyers inspect fit and confidence.
- Parent/youth safety context without glamorizing risk.
- Adult riding energy without making unsafe speed the brand center.

Avoid:

- Abstract gradient-only hero visuals.
- Stock-like dark atmosphere where the bike cannot be inspected.
- Reckless riding images for youth pages.
- Pure motocross-race identity that makes the brand feel like Hero instead of Guide.

## UI Direction

The website should feel like a decision cockpit, not a catalog wall.

| UI area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Header | Keep `Find Your Bike` or comparison action prominent. |
| Decision Engine | Highest-confidence module; visually more important than product browsing. |
| Product cards | Show fit, voltage, status, and proof readiness before price pressure. |
| Support pages | Calm, operational, and specific. |
| Policy pages | Dense, clear, and non-promotional. |
| Competitor pages | Editorial and source-aware, not attack pages. |

| YouTube channel avatar/banner | Brand can become a decision media brand. |
| Instagram/TikTok avatar | Short-name recall works in social contexts. |
| Email signature/support ticket | Brand feels reliable in after-sale support. |
| Helmet/apparel | Brand stretches into gear without feeling generic. |
| Bike frame/decal | Wordmark and icon survive real product use. |

| Packaging/battery/charger label | Brand supports operational trust and compliance. |

- OG/social preview crop review.
