# Ovkara Brand Clearance Workbench

## Purpose

This workbench defines the formal brand-clearance workflow for `Ovkara`.

It is not legal advice. It is the operating checklist used before public launch, logo rollout,
social-channel activation, paid traffic, trademark filing, or product-label production.

## Preliminary Read

`Ovkara` is the selected master brand and has been accepted by the owner for initial launch.

The name has three notable risk categories:

1. Legal and trademark availability across US and international databases.
2. Near-name visual or phonetic confusion, especially with `Vokèra / Vokera`.
3. Negative-meaning adjacency around `Ovčara / Ovcara`, which is not the same word but can appear
   in unaccented search contexts.

## Known Watch Items

| Item | Risk | Current read | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Vokèra / Vokera` | Visual/phonetic adjacency | Established UK/Ireland heating brand; different category but mature enough to monitor. | Keep `Ovkera` rejected; compare `Ovkara` visually and phonetically during attorney review. |
| `Ovkera` | Family-name conflict | Too close to `Vokera`. | Do not use as master brand, product line, or social handle. |
| `Ovkaro` | Defensive family name | Close to Ovkara and was available during naming review. | Consider defensive domain/handle if budget allows. |
| `Avkoro` | Backup family name | More separated from Vokera/Ovcara patterns. | Keep as backup only. |
| `Ovčara / Ovcara` | Negative historical/search adjacency | Not the same word, but close enough to require review. | Test pronunciation, spelling, and search-result associations before launch. |
| `Ovara`, `Ukara`, `Orkara`, `Omkara`, `Okara` | Near spelling or sound | Not automatic blockers. | Search exact, plural, phonetic, and class-related variants. |

## Negative Meaning And Pronunciation Test

Run a small US-market test before launch:

| Test | Method | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| Pronunciation | Ask at least 10 US English speakers to read `Ovkara` aloud. | Majority reads close to `ov-KAR-ah`; no dominant `ohv-CHAR-ah` reading. |
| Spelling recall | Say `Ovkara` once and ask people to spell it. | Misspellings are manageable and do not mostly become `Ovcara` or `Vokera`. |
| Association | Show the name without context and ask what category it feels like. | It can support modern outdoor / advisor / gear brand associations. |
| Negative search | Search `Ovkara`, `Ovcara`, `Ovčara`, and common misspellings. | No unavoidable first-page negative association for the exact brand. |

## Visual Identity Guardrails

Until clearance is complete:

- Do not make the `K` look like `C`.
- Do not make `OVKARA` visually resemble `VOKERA`.
- Avoid accent marks or European/Balkan visual cues.
- Use clear uppercase/lowercase wordmark testing: `Ovkara`, `OVKARA`, and `ovkara`.
- Test logo at favicon, app icon, product decal, helmet, apparel, YouTube avatar, and support-email sizes.
