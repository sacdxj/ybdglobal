## Visual Direction

The identity should feel:

- Guide / Navigator.
- Outdoor but not rustic.
- Premium but not luxury-only.
- Technical but not cold.
- Protective without being timid.
- Energetic without being reckless.

Use `OVKARA_VISUAL_IDENTITY_V1.md` as the current visual identity gate before approving the
provisional logo, favicon, color system, social previews, decals, or product-label use.

## Logo Test

The final logo must work in:

- Website header.
- Product decal.
- Helmet/apparel mark.
- YouTube thumbnail.
- Manual cover.
- Battery/charger label.
- Support ticket/email footer.
- Decision Engine UI.

Do not switch `PUBLIC_NOINDEX=false` or relax `robots.txt` until this gate is complete.
