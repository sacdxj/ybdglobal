## Strategic Shift

The site should no longer be judged as only an electric dirt bike ecommerce store.

The stronger frame is:

```text
An American electric dirt bike decision brand that helps riders and parents buy with confidence.
```

This means the brand sells confidence first, then products. Product pages, comparison pages,
guides, and support content should all help the buyer reduce the risk of choosing the wrong bike.

## Brand Mission

Help riders, parents, and practical buyers choose the right electric dirt bike with more
confidence, better evidence, and fewer expensive mistakes.

## Brand Vision

Become the trusted decision layer for electric dirt bike buyers in the United States, covering
rider fit, use case, safety, ownership cost, support, legality, and product comparison.

## Core Promise

```text
We help riders buy with confidence.
```

## Value Proposition

Choose an electric dirt bike by rider fit, terrain, power, safety, ownership support, and budget
instead of guessing from top speed, voltage, or brand hype.

## Brand Category

Primary category:

```text
Electric dirt bike decision brand
```

Functional categories:

- Buying guide and decision system.
- Product comparison and shortlist tool.
- Safety-aware parent education hub.
- Ownership and support confidence layer.
- Ecommerce path for qualified own-brand products.

## Target Audience

- Adult trail and off-road riders deciding which power class and chassis fit their use.
- Parents buying a youth or kids electric dirt bike and needing safety, size, supervision, and
  control guidance.
- Comparison shoppers evaluating Surron, Talaria, Razor, Stark, KTM, Honda, Yamaha, Segway, CAKE,
  Kuberg, and budget alternatives.
- Buyers who care about battery, range, charging, parts, warranty, shipping, financing, and repair.
- Dealer, distributor, or wholesale prospects when the intent is explicit.

## Buyer Questions The Brand Should Own

- Which bike fits this rider?
- Which bike fits my child?
- Which bike fits trail, farm, practice, or private-property use?
- Which claims are proven and which need verification?
- Which bike is legally usable for the intended riding plan?
- Which option has a realistic ownership path after checkout?
- Which choice gives the buyer confidence, not just excitement?

## Brand Personality

- Practical.
- Calm.
- Evidence-aware.
- Rider-first.
- Parent-safe.
- Technically clear.
- Honest about uncertainty.
- Commercial, but not pushy.

## Brand Voice

Use clear American English. Prefer direct buyer guidance, checklists, comparison tables, and
plain-language warnings. Do not sound like a hype-heavy powersports magazine or a thin affiliate
review site.

Good voice:

```text
Start with rider fit, then verify terrain, control, range, charging, and support.
```

Weak voice:

```text
Shop the hottest electric dirt bikes now.
```

## Naming Rules

The final brand name should:

- Prefer `.com`.
- Be 7-10 letters when possible.
- Be easy for an American buyer to pronounce.
- Avoid direct category words such as `dirt`, `volt`, `trail`, `ride`, and `electric` unless a
  strong availability reason exists.
- Not limit the future catalog to bikes only.
- Work for helmets, apparel, batteries, chargers, parts, service, youth safety, tools, content,
  and dealer programs.
- Avoid competitor confusion.
- Pass domain, trademark, pronunciation, spelling, and negative-meaning checks.

## Naming Workflow

1. Generate names from structured phonetic and semantic rules, not one-off guesses.
2. Remove names that are hard to pronounce, spell, remember, or say aloud.
3. Check `.com` availability.
4. Screen USPTO and common-law trademark risk.
5. Check search-result cleanliness and social handle conflicts.
6. Shortlist 20 names that can plausibly become an international consumer brand.

## Site Implications

- Homepage hero should lead with confidence, rider fit, and decision support.
- Navigation should treat guides, comparison, street-legal education, and support as brand assets,
  not secondary blog material.
- Product CTAs should remain available, but the primary brand promise should not be "we sell bikes."
- Commercial pages should prove fit and ownership confidence before pushing purchase.
- Comparison pages should act as objective decision tools and avoid forced own-brand claims.
- Product, legal, safety, shipping, warranty, financing, and range claims remain blocked until
  evidence is supplied.

## Launch Gate

The site can launch publicly only when:

- Final brand name and `.com` domain are chosen.
- Homepage and global chrome use the final brand identity.
- All public pages remove launch-blocking `DATA NEEDED` markers or are noindexed.
- Legal, youth safety, battery, warranty, shipping, financing, and product claims are reviewed.
- `astro.config.mjs` uses the real production domain.
- Dashboard evidence is described accurately, without claiming live SERP validation when cache is
  missing.
