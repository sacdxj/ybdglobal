# Naming Sprint V2: Domain-First Candidate Pool

## Why V2 Exists

Naming Sprint V1 produced strategically strong names, but the strongest candidates were already
blocked or risky at the exact `.com` level.

V2 changes the method:

```text
Strategy fit
-> invented form
-> exact .com availability
-> pronunciation
-> trademark/search
-> visual identity
```

The key change is domain-first filtering. A name is not serious until the exact `.com` survives
a live registrar/RDAP check.

## V2 Generation Rules

Prefer:

- 6-9 letters.
- 2-3 syllables.
- Smooth US pronunciation.
- Invented but not random.
- No direct bike/electric/trail/ride/dirt/volt cues.
- Fewer common endings such as `-aro`, `-ora`, `-ven`, `-ion`, and `-era`.
- Enough abstraction to support bikes, gear, parts, service, media, and decision tools.

Avoid:

- Obvious dictionary words.
- Famous-brand resemblance.
- Hard consonant clusters.
- Names that sound medical, crypto, gambling, supplements, or SaaS-only.
- Names that feel like a temporary Amazon private-label product.
