# Naming Sprint V1

## Purpose

This sprint turns the Decision Brand strategy into a first serious naming shortlist.

The goal is not to pick the final name today. The goal is to create a disciplined candidate pool
that can survive pronunciation, domain, trademark, search, visual identity, and expansion checks.

## Naming Thesis

The brand should feel like a trusted guide for electric dirt bike decisions, not a keyword store.

The name should suggest:

- Guidance.
- Clarity.
- Trust.
- Outdoor readiness.
- Technical confidence.
- Ownership support.

It should avoid:

- Dirt.
- Volt.
- Trail.
- Ride.
- Electric.
- Bike.
- Moto.
- Speed-only cues.
- Toy-like names.

## Current Constraint

Domain availability, USPTO status, social handles, and search cleanliness have not been checked
in real time. Do not buy a domain or approve a logo from this document alone.

## Initial Top 5

### 1. Arvion

Best fit for a premium decision brand. It feels modern, directional, and expandable without
sounding like a bike keyword.

Potential risk: needs conflict screening because it has a polished EV-like sound.

### 2. Norven

Strong Guide/Navigator fit. It feels trustworthy, northern, outdoor, and easy to say.

Potential risk: may feel slightly industrial unless visual identity warms it up.

### 3. Kairon

Clear, memorable, and strong. It can carry decision tools, products, media, and gear.

Potential risk: spelling may need reinforcement because buyers may hear `Kyron`.

### 4. Orvane

Premium, calm, and flexible. Good for a brand that wants to feel like an advisor rather than a
speed brand.

Potential risk: pronunciation should be tested with US buyers.

### 5. Veyro

Short, direct, and verification-adjacent. Works well with `Veyro Guide`, `Veyro Fit`, `Veyro
Check`, and `Veyro Support`.

Potential risk: very short names often have higher domain and trademark pressure.

## Naming Decision Rule

No candidate becomes final until it passes:

1. US pronunciation check.
2. Exact `.com` or acceptable bridge-domain check.
3. USPTO exact and similar-mark screening.
4. Google/common-law search.
5. Social handle scan.
6. Negative meaning scan.
7. Visual identity test.
8. Homepage headline test.
9. Product decal test.
10. Brand story fit.

## Recommended Next Action

Run a live availability and conflict screen for the Initial Top 20, then reduce to a Top 5
verified shortlist.
