# Electric Dirt Bike Decision Brand OS v2.0

## Purpose

This Brand OS turns the project from a keyword-led ecommerce site into a durable American
electric dirt bike decision brand.

The brand is not defined as:

```text
We sell electric dirt bikes.
```

The brand is defined as:

```text
We help riders buy with confidence.
```

This system should guide naming, domain selection, site architecture, content, SEO, product pages,
ads, YouTube, email, social, design, and AI production.

Version 2.0 adds the growth and moat layer:

```text
Brand DNA
-> Brand Archetype
-> Mental Availability
-> Brand Flywheel
-> Decision Engine
```

## Operating Principle

Every public brand asset must reduce buyer risk.

If a page, ad, product block, review, or guide does not help the buyer make a safer, clearer, more
confident decision, it is not yet doing the brand's job.

## How To Use This System

Use this sequence for major brand decisions:

```text
Brand foundation
-> positioning
-> audience
-> voice
-> brand DNA
-> archetype
-> mental availability
-> decision engine
-> naming and domain
-> visual identity
-> messaging
-> website pages
-> SEO/content
-> conversion
-> launch review
```

Do not start with a name, logo, domain, or homepage design. Those are outputs of the strategy.

Do not begin naming until the Brand DNA, Archetype, Mental Availability, Flywheel, and Decision
Engine modules have been reviewed. The strongest name should come from the strategic system, not
from keyword brainstorming.

## Current Project Status

The current Astro site already contains useful decision-brand foundations:

- Rider-fit logic.
- Parent safety logic.
- Voltage and power comparison.
- Street-legal caution.
- Ownership support pages.
- Competitor comparison boundaries.
- Dashboard-backed content architecture.

But it is not launch-ready because the final brand name, domain, product proof, policy proof,
legal review, safety review, and public test evidence are missing.

## Non-Negotiables

- Sell confidence before products.
- Put the rider before the spec.
- Act as the Guide / Navigator, not the hero.
- Build mental availability around real buying moments.
- Push the flywheel, not disconnected projects.
- Treat the Decision Engine as the long-term moat.
- Feed real buyer data back into content, rules, and homepage decisions.
- Do not claim what is not proven.
- Do not fake reviews, test data, support coverage, legal status, or product evidence.
- Do not publish competitor pages as attack pages.
- Do not use unsafe modification, bypass, or legal evasion content.
- Keep final naming separate from keyword SEO.
- Treat `.com` and trademark risk as launch gates.
