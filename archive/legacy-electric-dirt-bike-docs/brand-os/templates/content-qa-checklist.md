# Decision Brand Content QA Checklist

Page:

Reviewer:

Date:

## Decision Value

- [ ] The page answers a real buyer decision.
- [ ] The page puts rider fit before specs.
- [ ] The page explains tradeoffs.
- [ ] The page gives a clear next step.

## Persona Fit

- [ ] Target persona is clear.
- [ ] Buyer fears are addressed.
- [ ] Objections are answered.
- [ ] CTA matches buyer stage.

## Proof

- [ ] Product claims have source data.
- [ ] Speed claims have test method.
- [ ] Range claims have test method.
- [ ] Battery claims have source data.
- [ ] Warranty/shipping/financing claims have policy proof.
- [ ] Legal claims have official sources and review.
- [ ] Youth safety claims have safety review.

## Brand Voice

- [ ] Practical.
- [ ] Honest.
- [ ] Supportive.
- [ ] Not hype-heavy.
- [ ] No fake urgency.
- [ ] No unsupported "best" or "safest" claim.

## SEO

- [ ] Search intent is matched.
- [ ] Title and meta are clear.
- [ ] Internal links work.
- [ ] Schema matches visible content.
- [ ] No competitor leakage into product navigation.

## Launch Decision

- [ ] Publish.
- [ ] Publish as noindex test.
- [ ] Hold for proof.
- [ ] Hold for legal/safety/product review.
