## What Changes Now

The website may now use `Ovkara` in:

- Header.
- Footer.
- Metadata.
- Organization schema.
- Privacy page.
- Support page.
- Homepage brand language.
- Internal Brand OS documentation.

`Electric Dirt Bike` should remain a category and SEO phrase, not the brand name.

## What Does Not Change Yet

Domain purchase does not approve public launch.

The site should remain in private-review mode until these gates are complete:

- Trademark attorney review.
- USPTO, WIPO, EUIPO/TMview, common-law, and social-handle checks.
- Negative-meaning and pronunciation test for `Ovkara`.
- Final company/contact/support details.
- Approved product pricing, inventory, images, manuals, and fit claims.
- Approved shipping, warranty, returns, financing, parts, repair, and safety policies.
- Final logo and visual identity.
- Analytics and conversion tracking setup.
