# Mental Availability And Category Entry Points

## Purpose

SEO helps the brand appear when buyers search.

Mental availability helps the brand be remembered before the search.

The goal is:

```text
When a buyer faces an electric dirt bike decision, they think of the brand.
```

## Definition

A Category Entry Point, or CEP, is a moment, situation, need, worry, or trigger that makes a buyer
enter the category.

The brand should own these moments.

## Master CEP

```text
I need help choosing the right electric dirt bike.
```

## CEP Map

### 1. First-Time Buyer

Buyer thought:

```text
I want an electric dirt bike but do not know where to start.
```

Brand role:

Start with rider fit and use case.

Content:

- Homepage.
- Buying guides hub.
- Best electric dirt bikes.
- Beginner checklist.

CTA:

Start Your Buying Decision.

### 2. Parent Buying For A Child

Buyer thought:

```text
My child wants an electric dirt bike. I need to know what is safe and suitable.
```

Brand role:

Make age, size, control, supervision, and safety practical.

Content:

- Kids and youth guide.
- Ages 8-12 guide.
- 24V vs 36V vs 48V.
- Safety checklist.

### 3. Saw A Surron Or Talaria

Buyer thought:

```text
I saw a Surron/Talaria. Are there alternatives?
```

Brand role:

Turn competitor interest into objective comparison.

Content:

- Surron alternatives.
- Talaria alternatives.
- Adult performance guides.
- 60V/72V comparison.

### 4. Performance Upgrade

Buyer thought:

```text
I want something faster or more capable.
```

Brand role:

Explain power with control.

Content:

- Fast electric dirt bikes.
- 60V guide.
- 72V guide.
- Motor power guide.
- Range guide.

### 5. Farm Or Property Use

Buyer thought:

```text
I need quiet off-road mobility for land, ranch, or utility use.
```

Brand role:

Compare terrain, range, durability, maintenance, and support.

Content:

- Terrain-use guide.
- Range guide.
- Maintenance guide.
- Parts and repair.

### 6. Street-Legal Question

Buyer thought:

```text
Can I ride this on the road?
```

Brand role:

Provide cautious, document-first guidance.

Content:

- Street-legal guide.
- Registration and license guide.
- Dual-sport guide.

### 7. Budget And Price Anxiety

Buyer thought:

```text
How much should I spend, and what am I really paying for?
```

Brand role:

Explain total ownership value, not only sticker price.

Content:

- Price guide.
- Financing.
- Warranty.
- Parts and maintenance.

### 8. Battery, Range, Or Charging Anxiety

Buyer thought:

```text
How far will it go, and how hard is it to charge?
```

Brand role:

Explain real-world assumptions and verification.

Content:

- Range guide.
- Battery life guide.
- Charging guide.

### 9. Bike Broke Or Needs Parts

Buyer thought:

```text
Can I repair this or get parts?
```

Brand role:

Make ownership support visible.

Content:

- Parts and maintenance.
- Repair and service.
- Warranty and returns.

### 10. Gift Moment

Buyer thought:

```text
This could be a birthday or holiday gift, but I do not want to buy wrong.
```

Brand role:

Help gift buyers avoid unsafe or mismatched choices.

Content:

- Kids and youth.
- Beginner guide.
- Budget guide.
- Parent checklist.

### 11. YouTube Discovery

Buyer thought:

```text
I saw electric dirt bikes online and want to understand what is real.
```

Brand role:

Translate hype into decision criteria.

Content:

- Video explainers.
- Comparison pages.
- Range/speed proof guides.

### 12. Dealer Or Wholesale Inquiry

Buyer thought:

```text
Could I sell or support this category?
```

Brand role:

Show business readiness, product documentation, parts, and support workflow.

Content:

- Dealer inquiry.
- Product data sheets.
- Warranty and parts process.

## CEP Prioritization

Launch priority:

1. First-time buyer.
2. Parent buying for a child.
3. Saw a Surron or Talaria.
4. Street-legal question.
5. Battery/range anxiety.
6. Performance upgrade.
7. Price anxiety.

## Memory Structures

The brand should repeat these memory structures:

- "Buy with confidence."
- "Start with the rider."
- "Do not choose by voltage alone."
- "Do not choose by age alone."
- "Verify before road use."
- "Support matters after checkout."

## CEP Content Rule

Every major page should map to at least one CEP.

If a page does not map to a CEP, it is likely a weak page.

## CEP Tracking

Track:

- Organic queries by CEP.
- YouTube topics by CEP.
- Email signups by CEP.
- Product clicks by CEP.
- Support inquiries by CEP.
- Brand search growth after CEP content.

## Mental Availability Goal

The brand should become the remembered answer to:

```text
Who helps me choose the right electric dirt bike?
```
