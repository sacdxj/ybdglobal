# Brand DNA

## Purpose

Brand DNA is the non-negotiable pattern that should appear in every page, product experience,
video, email, support reply, ad, and tool.

It is the brand's internal genetic code.

## Core DNA Chain

```text
Trust
-> Clarity
-> Confidence
-> Guidance
-> Ownership
```

The brand should not publish anything that breaks this chain.

## 1. Trust

Trust means the brand earns belief before asking for action.

### What Trust Looks Like

- Claims are supported or marked as pending.
- Product specs show proof status.
- Legal pages use cautious language.
- Youth pages respect parent safety.
- Competitor pages are objective.
- Support policies are visible.

### Trust Failures

- Unsupported speed/range claims.
- Fake product reviews.
- Universal street-legal claims.
- Hiding warranty, parts, shipping, or repair details.
- Ranking products without criteria.

## 2. Clarity

Clarity means the buyer understands what matters and what to do next.

### What Clarity Looks Like

- Decision paths by rider, terrain, power, and budget.
- Tables that explain tradeoffs.
- "Best for" and "not ideal for" blocks.
- Clear noindex/hold status for incomplete content.
- Simple CTAs that match buyer stage.

### Clarity Failures

- Hype copy.
- Too many equal CTAs.
- Technical specs without explanation.
- Blog content that answers keywords but not buyer decisions.

## 3. Confidence

Confidence is the emotional outcome of the brand.

### What Confidence Looks Like

- The buyer knows which bike to shortlist.
- The parent knows what safety questions to ask.
- The rider knows which specs need verification.
- The comparison shopper understands whether an alternative is real.
- The buyer knows what happens after checkout.

### Confidence Failures

- The buyer still feels they are guessing.
- The page increases excitement but not decision quality.
- The page leads to purchase before resolving obvious risks.

## 4. Guidance

Guidance is the brand's behavior.

The brand should behave like a reliable guide, not a loud salesperson.

### What Guidance Looks Like

- Starts with the rider.
- Names tradeoffs.
- Warns when a decision is high-risk.
- Explains how to verify claims.
- Routes buyers to the next decision step.

### Guidance Failures

- Pushing every visitor to "Buy Now."
- Treating all buyers as the same.
- Avoiding uncomfortable safety, legal, or support questions.

## 5. Ownership

Ownership means the brand accepts that the product experience continues after purchase.

### What Ownership Looks Like

- Shipping, assembly, charging, maintenance, parts, warranty, repair, and returns are part of the
  buying decision.
- Product pages include ownership readiness.
- Support pages are clear, practical, and accountable.
- Post-purchase content feeds future trust.

### Ownership Failures

- Treating checkout as the end of the journey.
- Selling products without parts and warranty clarity.
- Publishing performance claims without maintenance and support context.

## DNA Review Questions

For every asset, ask:

1. Does this build trust?
2. Does this create clarity?
3. Does this increase confidence?
4. Does this guide the buyer?
5. Does this respect ownership after purchase?

If the answer is no to any item, the asset needs revision.

## DNA By Channel

### Homepage

Must express the full DNA chain within the first screen and first decision path.

### Product Pages

Must show trust, clarity, and ownership before conversion.

### Buying Guides

Must create clarity and confidence through decision frameworks.

### Comparison Pages

Must build trust by being fair and criteria-driven.

### YouTube

Must guide buyers through real decisions, not only entertain.

### Email

Must continue guidance, proof, and ownership confidence after the first visit.

### Support

Must make ownership feel accountable and human.

## DNA Scorecard

Score each asset 0-2:

| DNA Element | Score |
|---|---:|
| Trust | 0-2 |
| Clarity | 0-2 |
| Confidence | 0-2 |
| Guidance | 0-2 |
| Ownership | 0-2 |

Minimum public score:

```text
8/10
```

Automatic hold:

- Trust score below 2 on legal, youth, product, warranty, shipping, or performance pages.
