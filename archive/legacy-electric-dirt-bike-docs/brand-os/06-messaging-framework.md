```

```

## Product Page Message

Product pages must answer:

- Who is this bike for?
- Who is it not for?
- What evidence supports the specs?
- What claims still need verification?
- What should the buyer confirm before ordering?

Recommended modules:

1. Fit summary.
2. Best for / not ideal for.
3. Key verified specs.
4. Rider and terrain fit.
5. Power and control.
6. Range and charging method.
7. Shipping, warranty, parts, and service status.
8. Quote or buy readiness checklist.

## Buying Guide Message

Buying guides should teach decision methods.

Page angle:

```text
Here is how to decide, what to compare, and what to verify before buying.
```

Do not write generic informational content that could appear on any powersports blog.

## Comparison Page Message

Comparison pages should be objective, transparent, and non-attacking.

Use this structure:

1. What the buyer is really comparing.
2. Where the competitor is strong.
3. Where the buyer needs more proof.
4. Whether the current catalog has a true alternative.
5. Who should choose which path.
6. What to verify next.

Do not force an own-brand product into a comparison when it is not genuinely comparable.

## Street-Legal Message

Core message:

```text
Treat each bike as off-road until documents, equipment, and state rules prove otherwise.
```

Never imply universal street legality.

## Kids And Youth Message

Core message:

```text
Do not choose by age alone.
```

Always include:

- Rider size.
- Inseam/reach.
- Experience.
- Speed control.
- Brakes.
- Protective gear.
- Supervision.
- Riding area.
- Parent decision checklist.

## Support Message

Support pages should convert uncertainty into trust.

They must answer:

- How shipping works.
- What happens if the bike arrives damaged.
- What the warranty covers.
- How returns work.
- How parts are found.
- How repairs are handled.
- What information support needs from the buyer.

## Ad Messaging

### Google Search Ads

Use high-intent decision language:

- Choose the Right Electric Dirt Bike.
- Compare Electric Dirt Bikes by Rider Fit.
- Kids Electric Dirt Bike Buying Guide.
- 60V vs 72V Electric Dirt Bikes.
- Electric Dirt Bike Range and Ownership Guide.

Avoid:

- Cheapest.
- Fastest.
- Number one.
- Guaranteed legal.

### YouTube

Video positioning:

```text
Real buyer decisions explained clearly.
```

Example series:

- What to check before buying a kids electric dirt bike.
- 60V vs 72V explained for real riders.
- Why top speed is not the first spec to compare.
- Can electric dirt bikes be street legal?
- Surron alternatives: what buyers should compare.

### Email

Email should behave like an advisor.

Lifecycle:

- Welcome: Start with the rider.
- Guide: How to compare fit, power, and terrain.
- Proof: What to verify before checkout.
- Product: Shortlist models by use case.
- Support: Shipping, warranty, parts, and service confidence.

## Messaging Filters

Before publishing any message, ask:

- Does it reduce buyer risk?
- Does it put the rider before the spec?
- Is the claim proven?
- Does the CTA match the buyer's stage?
- Would this sound trustworthy to a cautious parent?
- Would this sound credible to an experienced rider?
