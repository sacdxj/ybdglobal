# SEO And Content Architecture

## SEO Role

SEO is not the brand. SEO is the demand capture system.

The brand should use SEO to meet buyers when they are confused, comparing, worried, or close to
purchase.

## Brand SEO Principle

The keyword can describe the page. It should not trap the brand.

Example:

```text
Brand: abstract final name
Page title: Electric Dirt Bike Buying Guide
Page role: decision support
```

## Content Pillars

### 1. Buying Decisions

Purpose:

Help buyers choose the right category, power system, budget, and product path.

Pages:

- Best electric dirt bikes.
- Price guide.
- Adult electric dirt bike guide.
- Beginner guide.
- Electric versus gas.

### 2. Rider Fit And Safety

Purpose:

Help parents and riders choose safely.

Pages:

- Kids electric dirt bikes.
- Ages 8-12.
- Teens and youth.
- 24V vs 36V vs 48V.
- Safety gear and supervision.

### 3. Power, Range, And Battery

Purpose:

Translate specs into real buying decisions.

Pages:

- 60V guide.
- 72V guide.
- Motor power guide.
- Range guide.
- Battery life guide.
- Charging guide.

### 4. Street Legal And Compliance

Purpose:

Help buyers understand road-use risk and documentation.

Pages:

- Street legal guide.
- Dual-sport guide.
- Registration and license guide.

Rule:

These pages require current official sources and legal/compliance review.

### 5. Comparisons And Alternatives

Purpose:

Capture competitor comparison demand without turning competitor names into product navigation.

Pages:

- Surron alternatives.
- Talaria alternatives.
- Razor alternatives.
- Stark VARG alternatives.
- KTM alternatives.
- Yamaha and Honda research pages.

Rule:

Competitor pages must be objective and source-reviewed.

### 6. Ownership Confidence

Purpose:

Support conversion by addressing ownership risk.

Pages:

- Shipping.
- Financing.
- Warranty and returns.
- Parts and maintenance.
- Repair and service.
- Contact.

## Page Type Rules

### Decision Guide

Used when the buyer needs a framework.

Must include:

- Decision map.
- Checklist.
- Tradeoffs.
- Internal links.
- Proof requirements.

### Collection Page

Used when the buyer is ready to compare products.

Must include:

- Product filters.
- Fit summary.
- Best for / not ideal for.
- Proof status.
- Support status.

### Product Page

Used when buyer evaluates a specific model.

Must include:

- Verified specs.
- Data source.
- Fit guidance.
- Claim boundaries.
- Shipping/warranty/support state.

### Comparison Article

Used for competitor demand.

Must include:

- Source notes.
- Criteria.
- Buyer scenario.
- No forced claim.
- Internal next step.

## Proof Rules

Public indexable pages cannot make unsupported claims about:

- Speed.
- Range.
- Battery capacity.
- Charging time.
- Warranty.
- Shipping.
- Financing.
- Street legality.
- Product age suitability.
- Rider weight limits.
- Dealer availability.
- Customer reviews.
- Test results.

Use `DATA NEEDED` during drafting. Remove or noindex before launch.

## Dashboard Relationship

The dashboard is the demand and routing layer.

It should be used for:

- Keyword universe.
- Topic clustering.
- Production priority.
- Merge/split decisions.
- Internal link suggestions.
- Content gap tracking.

It should not be described as live SERP validation unless live SERP evidence is actually present.

## SEO Launch Gate

Before launch:

- Final domain set in Astro config.
- Canonicals and sitemap verified.
- No launch-blocking `DATA NEEDED` on indexable pages.
- Legal and safety pages reviewed.
- Competitor pages source-checked.
- Product pages proof-complete or noindexed.
- Internal links pass.
- Schema matches visible content.
