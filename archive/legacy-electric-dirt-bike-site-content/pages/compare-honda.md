# Does Honda Make an Electric Dirt Bike?

Be careful with the phrase "Honda electric dirt bike." Honda sells motorcycles with electric
starters, but an electric starter does not make the motorcycle battery-powered.

## Electric Start vs Electric Power

Honda's current US dual-sport pages include combustion-engine motorcycles and may describe
electric starting. These are not electric dirt bikes.

Official source: [Honda Dual Sport lineup](https://powersports.honda.com/motorcycle/dual-sport)

Before publication, check Honda's current official US powersports catalog for a production
battery-electric off-road motorcycle. Do not treat a concept, children's ride-on, e-bike,
scooter, or gas bike with electric start as the requested product.

## FAQ

### Does "electric start" mean the bike is electric?

No. It refers to the engine-starting system, not the primary propulsion source.
