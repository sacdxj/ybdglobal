# Does Yamaha Make an Electric Dirt Bike?

Yamaha has developed and displayed electric motorcycle concepts and competition technology, but
a concept announcement is not the same as a currently available US production dirt bike.

## What Yamaha Has Announced

Yamaha announced the YE-01 electric motocross concept in 2025 and described an intention to
participate in the MXEP electric motocross category. The announcement identifies it as a concept
developed with Electric Motion.

Official source: [Yamaha YE-01 concept announcement](https://global.yamaha-motor.com/jp/news/2025/1106/eicma.html)

Before publication, recheck Yamaha's current US product catalog. Do not convert a concept,
prototype, race project, scooter, electric bicycle, or "electric start" gas motorcycle into a
retail electric dirt bike claim.

## What the Searcher May Mean

- A production electric motocross motorcycle.
- A Yamaha concept or race project.
- A gas dirt bike with electric starting.
- A Yamaha electric bicycle or scooter.
- An available electric dirt bike with Yamaha-like dealer expectations.

## FAQ

### Is the Yamaha YE-01 for sale in the US?

The cited Yamaha announcement describes a concept. Recheck Yamaha's current official US catalog
for production availability.

### Is electric start the same as an electric motorcycle?

No. "Electric start" commonly describes a starter motor on a combustion-engine motorcycle.
