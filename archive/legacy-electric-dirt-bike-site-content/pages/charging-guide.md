# Electric Dirt Bike Charging Guide

Use only the charger approved for the exact battery and follow the product manual. Connector
shape alone does not establish compatibility.

## Before Charging

1. Identify the exact model, battery, and charger.
2. Inspect the battery, cable, connector, and charger for visible damage or unusual condition.
3. Confirm the charging location and temperature meet the manual.
4. Keep the area clear and follow the supervision requirements.
5. Stop and contact support when the product displays a warning or behaves abnormally.

## Charger Compatibility

Never substitute a charger based only on plug fit. Verify:

- Exact model or battery part number.
- Approved charger part number.
- Input requirements.
- Output voltage/current.
- Connector and communication requirements.
- Applicable manual revision.

## Charging Location and Temperature

Follow the manual for ventilation, surface, temperature, moisture, supervision, storage, and
post-ride cooldown. Do not charge a battery that is damaged, unusually hot, wet beyond approved
conditions, or displaying a warning.

## Storage and Infrequent Use

Use the model manual for storage state of charge, inspection interval, temperature range, and
recharging schedule. Do not publish one universal storage percentage for every battery.

[Read Battery Life & Care](/buying-guides/battery-life/)

## Replacement Chargers

The support flow should request:

- Model and serial number.
- Battery and charger labels.
- Photos of connectors and any damage.
- Description of the issue and warning indicators.
- Purchase/order information where relevant.

Do not recommend third-party or higher-output chargers without written product approval.

## FAQ

### Can I use another charger if the plug fits?

No compatibility conclusion should be made from the plug alone. Use the exact approved charger.

### How long does charging take?

Use model-specific documented data. Battery state, temperature, capacity, charger, and product
limits affect time.

### Can I charge immediately after riding?

Follow the manual's temperature and cooldown guidance.

### What should I do if the battery or charger looks damaged?

Stop using it, keep it away from further charging or riding as directed by the manual, and
contact support. Do not open or modify the battery.
