## What Changes as Budget Increases?

Do not promise universal features at a price level. Instead, use the current catalog to explain
what actually changes:

- Product size and rider class.
- Battery, motor, controller, and charger.
- Brakes, suspension, wheels, and tires.
- Controls and riding modes.
- Verified performance and range evidence.
- Included equipment.
- Parts, warranty, and support.

### Very low price searches

At very low price points, the searcher may encounter toys, small ride-ons, used products, or
items that do not match an electric dirt bike buyer's intended class. Explain the distinction
before offering alternatives.

### Adult budget searches

Do not route an adult buyer to a youth or toy-class product simply because it fits the budget.
Show the rider and product-class mismatch clearly.

## How to Avoid False Economy

A cheaper bike may be poor value if it does not fit the rider, lacks parts, has unclear battery
documentation, cannot be supported, or requires replacement sooner. A higher price is not proof
of quality either. Compare evidence and ownership support.
