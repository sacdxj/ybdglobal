A pre-ride review may include tires, brakes, controls, fasteners, drive components, suspension,

technical approval.

## Fat Tires and Tire Selection

The keyword `fat tire electric dirt bike` belongs in this guide or a terrain/tire section, not a
thin standalone page. Tire width alone does not establish traction or terrain suitability.
Review compound, tread, pressure, wheel, load, clearance, surface, and product approval.

## Battery and High-Voltage Components

Do not open, rebuild, bypass, or modify battery, controller, motor, or charging components.
Route electrical-system faults through approved support.

## Parts Request Checklist

- Model and serial number.
- Order/purchase information.
- Part name or location.
- Photos of the product and labels.
- Description of wear, damage, or symptom.
- Previous modifications or repairs.
- Shipping destination.

## FAQ

### Are electric dirt bike parts universal?

No. Verify the exact model and revision.

### Can I use a charger with the same connector?

Connector fit does not prove electrical or communication compatibility. Use an approved charger.

### Where can I find service intervals?

Use the exact model manual and approved support documentation.
