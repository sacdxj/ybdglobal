## Start a Warranty Claim

The intake may require:

- Model and serial number.
- Order/proof of purchase.
- Description and timeline.
- Photos/video and warning codes.
- Maintenance history.
- Modification/repair history.
- Shipping location.

[DATA NEEDED: approved claim steps, response targets, authorization, parts/labor/shipping rules]

The final workflow should show these stages:

1. Claim received and ticket created.
2. Eligibility and safety triage.
3. Evidence request.
4. Approved troubleshooting only when appropriate.
5. Repair, part, replacement, refund, or denial decision under the policy.
6. Shipping/service instructions.
7. Resolution record and customer confirmation.

## Safety Issues

When a product may be unsafe, instruct the customer to stop use according to approved guidance
and contact support. Do not make the customer continue riding or charging to prove a fault.

## FAQ

### Are batteries covered?

Use the exact battery coverage, conditions, degradation definition, and exclusions.

### Are wear parts covered?

Use the approved policy; do not assume.

### Can I return an assembled or ridden bike?

Use the exact return condition rules.

### Do modifications affect coverage?

Use the approved warranty language for the product and claim.

### Who pays return or repair shipping?

Use the approved policy for the claim type, location, and authorization. Do not promise prepaid
shipping until the case is approved.
