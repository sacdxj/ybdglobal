motor.

## Chassis and Components

K2-48-MY-1417 uses an iron frame, spray paint finish, inverted aluminum front suspension,
domestic hydraulic rear shock absorber, 2-piston cast iron disc brakes, left-mounted LED
display, and optional lights. The saddle is four-layer elastic leather plus high-elasticity
sponge.

## FAQ

### Is the K2-48-MY-1417 good for adult riders?

It is an adult/youth candidate, but fit must be verified by dimensions, inseam, rider weight,
experience, and terrain. Do not present it as suitable for every adult.

### How does it differ from the 60V model?

The 60V MY-1417 keeps the same vehicle size and wheelbase but moves to a 60V 22.5Ah battery,
1500W rated motor, and 4000W maximum power.
