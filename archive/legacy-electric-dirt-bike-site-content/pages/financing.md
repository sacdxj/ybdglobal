# Electric Dirt Bike Financing

Financing may divide a purchase into payments, but approval, APR, fees, payment schedule, credit
impact, and refund handling depend on the provider and applicant.

## Before Applying

Review:

- Total purchase amount.
- Down payment.
- Number and timing of payments.
- APR and total financing cost.
- Fees and late-payment terms.
- Approval and credit implications.
- Shipping and tax treatment.
- Returns, cancellations, and refunds.

## Financing Is Not a Price Discount

Compare the total cost and payment obligation with the cash price. Do not describe financing as
free unless the approved provider terms support that exact claim.

## Compare Payment Options

| Option | Questions to ask |
|---|---|
| Pay in full | What is the complete delivered cost and refund method? |
| Promotional plan | When does the promotion end, and what happens afterward? |
| Installment financing | What are the APR, term, fees, payment dates, and total cost? |
| External payment method | What buyer protections and dispute rules apply? |

The store should not recommend a product class that the buyer cannot reasonably afford or hide
protective equipment, shipping, tax, and maintenance from the purchase estimate.

## FAQ

### Does everyone qualify?

No approval should be implied. Eligibility depends on the provider's process and terms.

### Does financing affect credit?

Use the provider's exact disclosure.

### What happens if I return the bike?

Follow both the store return policy and provider refund process.

### Is a monthly payment the total cost?

No. Review the number of payments, APR, fees, down payment, taxes, shipping, and other costs.
