# KTM Electric Dirt Bike Alternatives

KTM electric searches can refer to youth motocross products or adult off-road/dual-purpose
motorcycles. Identify the exact KTM model class before comparing an alternative.

## Current KTM Electric Product Context

KTM's US electric lineup includes kids motocross products and the FREERIDE E. Its official model
pages publish product-specific specifications, price, use, battery, charging, chassis, and
support information.

Official sources:

- [KTM Electric lineup](https://www.ktm.com/en-us/models/electric.html)
- [2027 KTM FREERIDE E](https://www.ktm.com/en-us/models/electric/freeride/2027-ktm-freeridee.html)

## Identify the Rider Class

- A parent researching an SX-E model needs a youth fit and safety comparison.
- An adult researching the FREERIDE E needs a full-size chassis, battery, charging, range/use,
  road-documentation, price, and dealer/support comparison.

Do not combine them into one generic KTM row.

## When an Own-Brand Alternative May Fit

An own-brand model may solve a different need: more manageable trail use, a lower price class,
another rider size, or a direct-to-consumer support path. State the difference rather than
implying equivalence.
