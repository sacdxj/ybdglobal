ride planning, ownership, budget, or legal use.

## Research Road Use Carefully

An electric dirt bike is not automatically street legal. Vehicle classification, paperwork,
equipment, intended use, and state/local rules all matter.

[Street-Legal Electric Dirt Bike Guide](/street-legal/)

## Use the Guides in This Order

1. Fit the rider.
2. Define terrain and riding location.
3. Choose a controllable product class.
4. Compare complete systems and verified test data.
5. Check price, delivery, support, parts, warranty, and legal use.
6. Open the product page only after the shortlist makes sense.
