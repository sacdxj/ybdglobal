1600 mm overall length and 1150 mm wheelbase as the 48V MY model, while using a 60V 22.5Ah

## FAQ

### Is 60V better than 48V?

Not automatically. The 60V model increases rated and maximum motor output, but fit, terrain,
charging time, support, price, and rider experience still decide whether it is the better bike.
