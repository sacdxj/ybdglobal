# CAKE Electric Off-Road Alternatives

CAKE's lineup spans lightweight off-road motorcycles and other electric motorcycle classes.
Identify the exact product and intended use before calling another electric dirt bike an
alternative.

## Lightweight Design Is a Use Decision

A lighter product may be easier to transport or manage in some terrain, but total fit also
depends on dimensions, geometry, suspension, brakes, wheels, tires, rider position, and intended
use. Do not present weight alone as proof that a bike is easier or safer.

## Road and Off-Road Versions

CAKE publishes both off-road and street-oriented product information. Keep the exact
configuration and market visible. A street-certified version in one market does not establish
US registration eligibility for every version.

## FAQ

### Is every CAKE bike an electric dirt bike?

No. CAKE publishes multiple product categories. Verify the exact model and intended use.

### Is a lower-cost bike a direct CAKE alternative?

Not necessarily. Product class, fit, chassis, design priorities, support, and use may differ.

### What should a CAKE shopper compare beyond design?

Compare physical fit, intended use, chassis, drive system, battery, charging, road/off-road
documentation, parts, service, warranty, and current availability.
