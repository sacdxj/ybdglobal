# Privacy Policy

This page is a launch draft. Replace it with the final privacy policy before publishing the
store under a real brand domain.

## Information We May Collect

The final policy should explain what information is collected when visitors browse products,
submit forms, place orders, request financing, or contact support.

## How Information May Be Used

The final policy should cover order handling, customer support, fraud prevention, shipping,
financing workflows, analytics, advertising measurement, and legal compliance.

## Third-Party Services

List the ecommerce platform, payment provider, shipping tools, analytics tools, email service,
advertising pixels, and any financing providers used by the final store.

## Customer Choices

Explain how customers can request access, correction, deletion, communication preferences, and
other privacy rights available in their location.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the current support channel until the final legal contact is approved:
[Contact Support](/support/contact/).
