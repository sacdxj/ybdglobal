## Product Catalog

For every SKU:

- Model name, SKU, price, inventory status and product class.
- Adult/youth designation and approved fit guidance.
- Dimensions, seat height, curb weight and rider-weight limit.
- Nominal/peak motor data with definitions.
- Battery voltage, capacity, chemistry and approved charger.
- Charging time and test conditions.
- Top speed and range with test method and conditions.
- Controller, drive, gearing, suspension, brakes, wheels and tires.
- Riding modes, speed limiting and parent-control features.
- Terrain/use restrictions and water/environmental ratings.
- Box contents, assembly requirements, manual and safety warnings.
- Product photos and usage rights.

## Commerce And Support

- Shipping coverage, cost rules, processing times and carriers.
- Delivery/assembly process and damage-claim workflow.
- Warranty terms, exclusions and claim process.
- Return, cancellation and refund policies.
- Parts catalog, compatibility data and service workflow.
- Financing provider, eligibility, APR/fee disclosures and refund handling.
- Real dealer/service locations, if any.

## Street-Legal Evidence

- VIN/title/MSO documentation by model.
- Equipment list by model.
- Exact claim the company is prepared to make.
- Official federal/state/local sources with review dates.
- Named legal/compliance reviewer.

No model should be labeled `street legal`, `dual sport`, `40+ MPH`, or suitable for a
specific child age until the corresponding evidence is present.
