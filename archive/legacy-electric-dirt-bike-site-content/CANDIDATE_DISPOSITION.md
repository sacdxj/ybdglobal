## Net Result

All 56 keyword candidates now have a defined destination:

- a distinct commercial, buying, ownership, support, legal, or Blog page;
- a stronger parent page or on-page section when standalone intent is too thin;
- or a review hold when legal evidence, product eligibility, or competitor intent is unclear.

The architecture also adds the non-keyword pages a real store needs, including Home, Buying
Guides Hub, Shipping, Warranty/Returns, Contact, Battery Care, and a reusable product template.

This produces fewer URLs than the raw workbook, but each remaining URL has a distinct job
and enough content depth to deserve indexing.
