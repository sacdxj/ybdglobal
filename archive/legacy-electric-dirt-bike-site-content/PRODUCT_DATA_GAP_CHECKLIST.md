# Product Data Gap Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist tracks what is still missing before the current electric dirt bike catalog can
move from SEO/product drafts to publishable ecommerce pages.

Current product count: 9 draft SKUs.

- X1-60-MY-1417

## Launch Blockers

These items should be completed before any product page is treated as a live sales page.

| Gap | Why it matters | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Final brand name and model naming | Product titles, URLs, packaging, support, warranty, and schema need one approved naming system | All SKUs |
| Retail price and compare-at policy | Source sheets show factory-style prices, not customer-facing US retail prices | All SKUs |
| SKU/variant records | Commerce, inventory, fulfillment, warranty, and analytics require exact SKU IDs | All SKUs |
| Inventory and availability | Product cards should not imply availability without stock status | All SKUs |
| Real product photos and videos | Static site design and product conversion need authentic media, not only table data | All SKUs |
| Product manual | Safety, assembly, charging, maintenance, warnings, and support claims need a source | All SKUs |
| Warranty, returns, and support policy | Product and Offer schema cannot be complete without commercial policy | All SKUs |
| Shipping scope and carton handling | Carton dimensions exist, but US shipping regions, carriers, cost, and delivery windows are missing | All SKUs |
| Speed/range test method | Current speed/range numbers are source-table values without public test conditions | All SKUs |
| Rider fit guidance | Age/height/inseam/weight/experience guidance is not yet approved | All SKUs |
| Compliance and legal status | No product should claim street legal without exact documentation | All SKUs |
