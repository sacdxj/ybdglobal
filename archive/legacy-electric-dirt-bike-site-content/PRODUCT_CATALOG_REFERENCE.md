## Shared Equipment

- Motor type: mid-mounted motor.
- Climbing degree: at least 65 degrees in the source table; this needs engineering review before
  public use because the value is unusually high for consumer copy.
- Charger: 3A CE-certified or 5A CE-certified charger option.
- Charging time: 48V models show 5h with 3A or 4h with 5A; 60V/72V models show 9h with 3A or
  5.4h with 5A.
- Display: left-mounted LED display.
- Headlight/taillight: optional.
- Frame: iron frame.
- Saddle: four-layer elastic leather plus high-elasticity sponge.
- Export package: oil-film carton plus EPE foam.
- Colors: Black, Red, Orange, Customization.
- Lead time: 30 days.
- 40HQ quantity: 213 pcs for the first four models; 159 pcs for K2-72-LZ-1719.

## G1 Shared Equipment

- Motor type: wheel-hub motor.
- Battery type: lead-acid battery.
- Charger: 1.5A lead-acid battery charger.
- Charging time: 4-5 hours.
- Display: LCD display.
- Headlight: adaptive headlight.
- Frame: iron frame.
- Surface treatment: powder coating.
- Suspension: none listed for front or rear.
- Brake system: disc brake.
- Seat: four-layer elastic leather plus high-elasticity sponge.
- Packaging: carton packaging.
- Color: orange body with black saddle.
- Lead time: 30 days.
- 40HQ capacity: 766 units.

## X1 Shared Equipment

- Motor type: mid-mounted motor.
- Charger: 5A CE-certified charger.
- Display: center-mounted LED display.
- Headlight/taillight: none listed.
- Frame: aluminum alloy.
- Surface treatment: powder coating.
- Front suspension: inverted aluminum-alloy front suspension.
- Rear suspension: domestic hydraulic shock absorber with external gas-oil separation note.
- Brake system: 2-piston cast iron disc brakes with hydraulic dual-disc note.
- Saddle: four-layer elastic leather plus high-elasticity sponge.
- Export package: oil-film carton plus EPE foam.
- Colors: Black, Red, Orange, Customization.
- Lead time: 30-45 days.

## Publication Rules

1. Use K2/X1 model codes and the G1 working name until the final own-brand naming system is supplied.
2. Treat speed as km/h unless a verified mph conversion and test method is approved.
3. Keep competitor brand names out of collection and product pages.
4. Keep street-legal claims out of product pages unless the exact model has compliant paperwork.
5. Do not use the climbing-degree value in strong marketing claims before technical review.
6. Replace placeholders only with facts from the K2, G1, X1 workbooks or approved product records.
