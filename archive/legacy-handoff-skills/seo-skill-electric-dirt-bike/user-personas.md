# Electric Dirt Bike User Personas

## 1. Adult Performance Rider

Needs:

- speed, voltage, torque, range, suspension, brakes, tires, and terrain fit
- realistic expectations about top speed and range
- parts, warranty, support, shipping, and financing confidence

Searches:

- `electric dirt bike for adults`
- `fastest electric dirt bike`
- `72v electric dirt bike`
- `best electric dirt bike`

Content angle:

- performance tradeoffs
- rider size and terrain fit
- 72V vs 60V decision logic
- own-brand collection CTA

## 2. Parent Buyer

Needs:

- age fit, speed control, low-voltage safety, protective gear, supervision, and beginner mistakes
- confidence that the page is not encouraging unsafe riding

Searches:

- `electric dirt bike for kids`
- `electric dirt bike for 10 year old`
- `electric dirt bike for teens`
- `mini electric dirt bike`

Content angle:

- age/rider-fit checklist
- safety-first recommendations
- gear and supervision reminders
- kids/youth collection CTA

## 3. Street-Legal Researcher

Needs:

- clear explanation that rules vary by state/local area
- checklist for lights, mirrors, VIN, registration, insurance, license, and road-use limits

Searches:

- `street legal electric dirt bike`
- `are electric dirt bikes street legal`
- `do you need a license for an electric dirt bike`

Content angle:

- cautious legal education
- official-source requirement
- compliance-review flag
- street legal guide CTA

## 4. Comparison Shopper

Needs:

- objective alternatives to Surron, Razor, Talaria, Stark, KTM, Segway, Yamaha, or Honda
- comparison without competitor-bashing
- path back to own-brand products

Searches:

- `surron electric dirt bike`
- `razor electric dirt bike`
- `talaria electric dirt bike`
- `surron alternative`

Content angle:

- scenario-based comparison
- buyer fit table
- own-brand alternative CTA

## 5. Parts And Support Buyer

Needs:

- batteries, chargers, controllers, brakes, tires, suspension, chains, sprockets, helmets, maintenance, repair, and warranty support

Searches:

- `electric dirt bike parts`
- `electric dirt bike charger`
- `electric dirt bike battery`
- `electric dirt bike repair`

Content angle:

- maintenance checklist
- compatibility caution
- support/contact CTA

Needs:
