# Title And Meta Optimization

## Title Rules

- Put the primary keyword or intent early.
- Match the page type: buyer guide, comparison, street-legal guide, parts guide, collection.
- Avoid unsupported claims like `best range`, `fastest`, `guaranteed street legal`, or `official`.
- Keep it clear before clever.

## Patterns

- `Electric Dirt Bike for Adults: Buyer Guide`
- `Electric Dirt Bike for Kids: Age and Safety Guide`
- `72V vs 60V Electric Dirt Bikes: What to Know`
- `Street Legal Electric Dirt Bikes: US Guide`
- `Surron Alternative: Electric Dirt Bike Comparison`
- `Electric Dirt Bike Battery Life: What Affects Range`

## Meta Rules

- Describe the real value of the page.
- Mention rider fit, speed, range, battery, safety, support, or comparison when relevant.
- Do not promise specs or outcomes not proven by sources.
