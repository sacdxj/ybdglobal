# Technical SEO Method

## URL

- lowercase
- hyphen-separated
- short and readable
- no dates unless time-sensitive
- no competitor name in own-brand collection URL

## Schema

- Article for guides and comparisons.
- FAQPage only when FAQ is visible.
- HowTo only when visible steps exist.
- BreadcrumbList for hierarchy.
- Product/Offer only for real product pages with visible required data.

## Internal Links

Link back to relevant own-brand pages:

- Shop All
- Adult Dirt Bikes
- Kids & Youth
- Street Legal
- Buying Guides
- Shipping & Delivery
- Financing Plans
- Contact / Dealer Support

Competitor articles must link back to own-brand commercial pages.

## Launch QA

Check indexability, canonical, sitemap, robots, crawlable links, title/meta uniqueness, H1, mobile rendering, image alt text, structured data validity, and page speed.
