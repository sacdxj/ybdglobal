# Content Outline Method

Use after business-fit and SERP intent are clear.

## Outline Rules

- Open with a direct answer.
- Follow the buyer's decision path.
- Use H2s for major decisions and H3s for supporting details.
- Include tables/checklists where they reduce uncertainty.
- Add proof notes before drafting claims.
- End with a natural next step.
