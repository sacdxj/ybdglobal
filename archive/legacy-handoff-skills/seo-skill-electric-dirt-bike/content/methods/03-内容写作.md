# Content Writing Method

Use for drafting Electric Dirt Bike SEO pages.

## Writing Rules

- Write in US English unless the user requests another language.
- Be practical, direct, and safety-aware.
- Avoid generic powersports filler.
- Use `[DATA NEEDED: ...]` for missing product, legal, warranty, shipping, financing, dealer, or support facts.
- Do not invent tests, reviews, customer cases, specs, or legal certainty.
- Explain tradeoffs: speed vs control, voltage vs rider skill, range vs terrain, price vs support.
- Add an own-brand next step only after the article earns trust.

## Required Blocks For Sensitive Topics

- Street legal: state that rules vary by state/local area and require official review.
- Kids/youth: include supervision, gear, speed/voltage caution, and parent decision criteria.
- Battery: avoid unsafe modification instructions.
- Comparison: be objective and scenario-based; do not attack competitors.
