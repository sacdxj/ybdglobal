# SERP Analysis Method

Use for current SERP review, brief creation, or competitor-gap analysis.

## Steps

1. Search the keyword in US English context when live research is needed.
2. Record dominant SERP pattern: ecommerce category, product listing, listicle, guide, comparison, forum/social, legal explainer, mixed.
3. Record the top competitors and their strengths.
4. Identify gaps: rider fit, safety, voltage, realistic range, parts/support, shipping/financing, legal caveats, internal next step.
5. Decide page type.
6. Map PAA and related questions only if they serve the buyer path.
7. Define information gain and proof requirements.
