# Electric Dirt Bike Content Analysis

## Strategy Model

Use the Zhutova-style growth system, adapted to bike:

```text
Product positioning
-> keyword boundary
-> site structure
-> page opportunity pool
-> Build now / Build later priority
-> article brief
-> article production
-> social content columns
-> data feedback
```

## Content Pillars

- adult performance and speed
- kids/youth safety
- street-legal education
- 72V vs 60V, battery, range and charging
- comparison and alternatives
- maintenance, parts and support
- shipping, financing and purchase confidence

## Audit Questions

- Does this topic help a real electric dirt bike buyer decide?
- Is the page commercial, educational, comparison, support, or review?
- What exact proof is needed before publication?
- What must not be claimed?
- Which own-brand collection, guide, support, or contact path should the reader visit next?
