# Electric Dirt Bike SEO and Business-Fit Guide

## Data Integrity Rules

Use only local project sources for brand/product-specific facts. Do not invent specs, tests, legal status, warranty, shipping, financing, dealer, inventory, reviews, or customer cases.

If the source is missing, mark:

```markdown
[DATA NEEDED: exact fact required]
```

## Positioning

Electric Dirt Bike helps US buyers choose electric dirt bikes by rider fit, terrain, speed, voltage, range, safety, budget, and support needs.

Do not position the site as a generic e-bike blog, motorcycle news site, or competitor directory.

## Core Topic Universe

Build content around:

- adult performance electric dirt bikes
- kids/youth safety and age fit
- street legal and dual-sport education
- 72V vs 60V, range, battery, charging, and motor power
- Surron/Razor/Talaria/Stark/KTM alternatives
- budget-friendly buying guides
- parts, accessories, maintenance, warranty and support
- shipping, financing, dealer and wholesale inquiry paths

## Out-of-Scope Gate

Reject or manual-review:

- pure pedal bikes
- generic motorcycles
- ATVs, scooters, mobility scooters
- toy-only terms with no electric dirt bike fit
- unsafe modifications, battery hacking, limiter bypasses
- coupons, login, tracking, entertainment, adult, medical, illegal traffic

## Page Roles

Choose one:

- `collection page`
- `product-support page`
- `buying guide`
- `comparison article`
- `street-legal guide`
- `battery guide`
- `parts/support guide`
- `FAQ/section`
- `parent-page section`
- `review`

## Title and Meta Guidance

Title patterns:

- `Electric Dirt Bike for Adults: Buyer Guide`
- `72V vs 60V Electric Dirt Bikes: What to Know`
- `Street Legal Electric Dirt Bikes: US Guide`
- `Surron Alternative: Electric Dirt Bike Comparison`

Meta pattern:

- `Compare rider fit, speed, range, battery, safety and support before choosing an electric dirt bike.`

## Conversion Actions

Use one relevant CTA:

- Shop Electric Dirt Bikes
- Compare Adult Models
- Find a Kids Bike
- Check 72V vs 60V Options
- Read the Street Legal Guide
- Ask About Shipping
- Contact Dealer Support

Do not promise response time, financing approval, warranty coverage, shipping dates, or legal status unless sourced.
