# SEO Fundamentals For Electric Dirt Bike

## Core Principle

Match search intent first, then add buyer-specific information gain. Do not write generic powersports content that only repeats common advice.

## Search Intent Classes

- `commercial/product`: for sale, buy, best, price, collection, product comparison
- `adult performance`: adults, fast, speed, 72V, 60V, motor, suspension
- `kids/youth safety`: kids, youth, teens, age-specific, mini, low voltage
- `street-legal/how-to`: street legal, registration, license, dual sport
- `comparison`: vs, alternative, Surron, Razor, Talaria, Stark, KTM
- `battery/range`: battery life, range, charging, voltage, charger
- `parts/support`: parts, repair, maintenance, warranty, accessories
- `dealer/wholesale`: dealer, distributor, wholesale, bulk
- `navigation/review`: brand navigation, official lookup, mixed intent

## Information Gain

Add at least one of:

- rider-fit decision logic
- terrain-use comparison
- voltage/speed/range tradeoff
- age/safety checklist
- official-source legal caveat
- parts/support risk reduction
- own-brand collection or support path

## Schema Eligibility

- `Article`: guides, comparisons, explainers.
- `FAQPage`: only with visible FAQ.
- `HowTo`: only visible step-by-step procedures.
- `BreadcrumbList`: production pages with clear hierarchy.
- `Product`: only specific product pages with real visible product details.
- `Offer`: only if price, availability, currency, and purchase details are visible.

## Technical Launch QA

Confirm indexability, canonical, sitemap inclusion, crawlable internal links, unique title/meta/H1, mobile rendering, image alt text, structured data validity, and no noindex/404/redirect/canonical issues.
