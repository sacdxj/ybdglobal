# Electric Dirt Bike SEO Content Checklist

Use this checklist before approving briefs, outlines, drafts, or production pages.

## Automatic Fail If Any Are True

- No business-fit classification.
- Unrelated bicycle, motorcycle, ATV, scooter, mobility, toy-only, coupon, login, tracking, adult, medical, illegal, or entertainment intent treated as Build now.
- Unsafe modification, limiter bypass, battery hacking, or legal evasion content.
- Unsupported product spec, top speed, range, battery, warranty, shipping, financing, dealer, review, customer, or legal claim.
- Street-legal content lacks source/reviewer caveat.
- Competitor name is used as own-brand collection/product navigation.
- Search intent mismatches the page type.
- No direct answer near the opening.
- No Electric Dirt Bike-specific information gain.
- No internal-link plan.
- Schema recommendation does not match visible content.
- Writing supervisor score is below 75.

## 1. SERP And Intent

- SERP or workbook evidence reviewed.
- Dominant result type identified.
- Page format matches the query.
- PAA/related questions are mapped only when useful.
- Competitor gaps are documented.
- Unique buyer value is defined.

## 2. Search Intent Resolution

- Primary job-to-be-done is clear.
- Searcher stage is identified.
- Decision blockers are answered.
- Reader knows the next step after reading.
- At least four relevant depth assets are included where needed.

## 3. E-E-A-T And Proof

- Trust goal, experience goal, expertise goal, authority goal, evidence plan, and risk boundary are written before drafting.
- Brand/product proof is mapped.
- External proof is mapped for legal/safety/battery claims.
- Source-to-claim map exists for important claims.
- Examples are labeled real, anonymized, or illustrative.
- Reviewer need is documented.
- Publish strength is capped when proof or independent review is missing.

## 4. Structure

- One H1.
- H2s follow the buyer decision path.
- H3s support details, not filler.
- Tables/checklists are used where they clarify choices.
- FAQ is visible if FAQPage schema is recommended.

## 5. Technical SEO

- Title is unique and accurate.
- Meta description is accurate.
- URL slug is short, lowercase, and hyphenated.
- Internal links are crawlable and descriptive.
- Schema matches visible content.
- Page is indexable and canonical is correct.
- Mobile rendering and page speed are checked.
