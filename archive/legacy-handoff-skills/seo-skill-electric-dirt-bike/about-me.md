# About Electric Dirt Bike

## Positioning

Electric Dirt Bike is a US independent ecommerce project for electric dirt bikes.

The SEO positioning is derived from the cleaned keyword workbook, page opportunity pool, and dashboard architecture. Do not replace this with a generic brand strategy unless the user provides real brand/product evidence.

It helps buyers:

- choose electric dirt bikes by rider fit, age, terrain, speed, voltage, range, and budget
- compare adult, kids/youth, high-speed, 72V, 60V, and street-legal options
- understand battery, charging, maintenance, parts, warranty, shipping, financing, and support considerations
- compare alternatives such as Surron, Razor, Talaria, Stark, KTM, Segway, Yamaha, and Honda without turning competitor names into own-brand navigation

## Core Promise

Reduce purchase uncertainty for electric dirt bike buyers by connecting practical product education, safety-aware buying guidance, and clear next steps to own-brand collections or support.

Use cautious wording:

- Good: `This guide helps buyers compare rider fit, voltage, range, speed, safety, and support before choosing an electric dirt bike.`
- Avoid: `This bike is guaranteed street legal everywhere and has the best range in the market.`

## Primary Audience

- adult performance riders
- parents buying kids/youth bikes
- beginners choosing a first electric dirt bike
- comparison shoppers
- street-legal and dual-sport researchers
- parts/support buyers
- dealer, distributor, or wholesale prospects

## Core SEO Themes

- electric dirt bike
- electric dirt bike for adults
- electric dirt bike for kids
- street legal electric dirt bike
- 72V and 60V electric dirt bikes
- speed, range, battery and charging
- Surron/Razor/Talaria alternatives
- electric dirt bike parts and maintenance
- shipping, financing, warranty, dealer and wholesale

## Content Boundaries

Do not chase traffic from unrelated bicycles, generic motorcycles, ATVs, scooters, mobility devices, toy-only queries, entertainment, adult, medical, illegal, coupon, login, tracking, or unsafe modification searches.

## Tone

Write like a practical buyer advisor:

- direct
- specific
- safety-aware
- commercially useful
- honest about tradeoffs
- careful with specs, legal, warranty, shipping, and range claims
